     Experimental Multiplicity-Driven
  Reconstruction Algorithm (EMDRA):
 A Recursive, Quantum-Enhanced Platform
         for Industrial Innovation
            Developed in collaboration with SKC Solutions LLC
                                       April 2025


                                        Abstract
        This paper introduces EMDRA, a recursive, tensor-based framework for re-
     constructing incomplete experimental processes, with an initial focus on platinum
     recovery from steel slag. By integrating Lie-algebraic hypothesis generation, non-
     Markovian memory modeling, quantum corrections, and real-time digital twins,
     EMDRA establishes a modular, extensible platform applicable across industrial
     recovery, circular economy, and climate technology sectors.

1     Introduction
Reviving incomplete or partially documented experimental processes presents a formidable
challenge. The platinum recovery project at SKC Solutions LLC, based on legacy en-
gineering discoveries, exemplifies this problem. Traditional trial-and-error methods are
insufficient; instead, a structured, recursive, and cyber-physical approach is necessary.
We propose EMDRA to meet this challenge.


2     Core Framework
2.1    Prime-Indexed Tensor Encoding (PIT)
Known experimental steps are encoded as prime-indexed tensor states:
                                    X
                               T0 =     pi · State(pi )                              (1)
                                          pi ∈P


2.2    Recursive Tensor Expansion (RTE)
Missing experimental steps are hypothesized via recursive tensor mutations:
                                         X
                              Tt+1 = Λm      pαi Tt + F (t)                          (2)
                                             pi ∈PN


where F (t) represents hypothesized transformations.

2.3    Multiplicity Feedback Weighting (MFW)
Hypotheses are dynamically weighted based on chemical feasibility, energy favorability,
and experimental feedback.


3     Key Enhancements
3.1    Lie-Algebraic Hypothesis Generation
Operators {Om } are defined for chemical transformations. Candidate hypotheses are
generated by symbolic commutators:

                                 [Om , T0 ] = Om T0 − T0 Om                          (3)

Valid reactions are filtered via chemical plausibility checks.

4     Digital Twin Architecture
A real-time digital twin integrates EMDRA’s recursive hypotheses with live sensor data.
    • Edge Devices: IoT integration (pH, temperature sensors).
    • Cloud Layer: DFT and PIMC calculations.
    • AR Visualization: Unity-based overlays for technician guidance.

6     Roadmap
    1. Develop Lie-algebraic operator library using SymPy and RDKit.
    2. Implement fractional differential DTO solver (Julia).
    3. Prototype digital twin MVP (Unity + Aspen Plus).
    4. Deploy GNN-based hypergraph on edge devices.


7     Applications Beyond Platinum Recovery
    • Rare Earth Element (REE) recycling
    • Battery material recovery
    • CO2 mineralization
    • Synthetic biology pathway reconstruction


8     Conclusion
EMDRA offers a new paradigm for industrial experimental reconstruction: recursively
evolving hypotheses, memory-aware modeling, quantum-corrected feedback, and real-
time digital twin optimization. Phase I focuses on platinum recovery; Phase II extends
to circular economy applications.
