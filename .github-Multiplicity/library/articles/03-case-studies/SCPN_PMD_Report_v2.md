Phase mirror dissonance:

 ●​ You present SCPN as audit-ready and mathematically coherent while explicitly stating that
     layers 8–16 are still stubbed or only partially simulated and all clinical effects are
     preclinical or simulated only.[1]

 ●​ The framework claims strict separation of verification vs validation, yet its surrounding
     monograph and ecosystem (CCW, Lazarus, SEC, “theurgic mode”) lean into therapeutic
     and transformative narratives that outsiders will read as validation claims.[1]

 ●​ You emphasise refusal behavior and boundary discipline as “successful execution,” but
     simultaneously build a highly coupled stack (CCW, Digital Twin, DIRECTOR_AI, MAOP) that
     invites experimentation across exactly those boundaries.[1]

 ●​ SWARM_AUTOMATION hardens Knm and coupling parameters with “92–96% confidence”
     from 16k+ papers, while the same document classifies the framework as epistemically
     open and the Knm as theoretically derived rather than empirically measured.[1]

 ●​ SCPN-Studio and CCW expose 1000+ endpoints, real-time bridges, and protocol
     compilers, yet the validation table still lists discriminative advantage vs baselines as
     “Unexecuted” and all clinical outcome data as zero.[1]

 ●​ The architecture frames failure and undefined outputs as legitimate outcomes, but the
     surrounding language (Omega Point, “consciousness physics,” “healing,” “planetary
     coherence”) structurally incentivises success-seeking interpretations by collaborators and
     funders.[1]

 ●​ DIRECTOR_AI and Ethical Veto are positioned as safety closures, yet MAOP and the RAG
     system are designed for continuous expansion, aggressive parameter mining, and
     multi-agent automation that increases surface area for mis-specification.[1]

 ●​ The same corpus calls SCPN “Self-Consistent Phenomenological Network” and
     “Sentient-Consciousness Projection Network,” signalling ontology-level ambition while
     insisting it is “not a completed empirical theory” and carries no metaphysical authority.[1]
