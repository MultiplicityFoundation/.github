1\. Cover Sheet

Title of the Project

Institutional Information

Project Duration

2\. Project Summary

Overview: Brief description of the project, including objectives and
significance.

Intellectual Merit: Explanation of the potential to advance knowledge.

Broader Impacts: Potential benefits to society and contributions to
desired societal outcomes.

3\. Table of Contents

Automatically generated by the NSF FastLane system.

4\. Project Description

Introduction: Background and context of the research problem.

Objectives: Clear and concise statement of the research goals.

Literature Review: Summary of relevant research and how the proposed
work will build on it.

Methodology: Detailed description of the research design, methods, and
procedures.

Preliminary Results: Any preliminary data or findings that support the
feasibility of the project.

Work Plan: Timeline and milestones for the project.

Broader Impacts: Detailed explanation of how the project will benefit
society, including educational outreach and diversity initiatives.

Evaluation and Dissemination: Plans for assessing the project's success
and sharing results with the broader community.

5\. References Cited

Comprehensive list of all references cited in the project description.

6\. Biographical Sketches

PI and Co-PIs: Two-page biosketches for each, including education,
appointments, and relevant publications.

7\. Budget and Budget Justification

Detailed Budget: Breakdown of costs, including personnel, equipment,
travel, and other expenses.

Budget Justification: Explanation of the need for each budget item.

8\. Current and Pending Support

List of current and pending support for the PI and Co-PIs.

9\. Facilities, Equipment, and Other Resources

Description of the resources available to support the project.

10\. Data Management Plan

Plan for managing and sharing data generated by the project.

11\. Postdoctoral Mentoring Plan

Required if the proposal includes funding for postdoctoral researchers.

12\. Supplementary Documents

Letters of Collaboration: Letters from collaborators confirming their
participation.

Other Documents: Any additional documents required by the specific
program solicitation.

13\. Appendices

Any additional information that supports the proposal but is not
included in the main sections.

Tips for Success:

Follow the Guidelines: Ensure compliance with the NSF Proposal and Award
Policies and Procedures Guide (PAPPG) 1.

Clear and Concise Writing: Make sure your proposal is well-organized and
easy to read.

Highlight Innovation: Emphasize the novelty and significance of your
research.

Broader Impacts: Clearly articulate how your project will benefit
society.
