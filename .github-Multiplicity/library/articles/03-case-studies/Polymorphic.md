         Polymorphic Multiplicity Density Matrices (PMDMs) extend the foundation of Multiplicity Theory
         by incorporating prime-based encoding, recursive feedback, and hypergraph dynamics to model
         multi-scale, adaptive computation. This work formalizes PMDMs mathematically, linking them to
         quantum computation, neuromorphic AGI, and self-proving conjectures.

matrices. PMDMs are proposed as an evolution of this paradigm, integrating tensor networks, recursive feedback
mechanisms, and hypergraph-based structures for adaptable, real-time learning. This paper explores their mathematical
formulation and applications.
