### Prime-Based Encryption

Prime-based encryption in multiplicative computing harnesses the unique
properties of prime numbers, particularly their indivisibility by any
other number except 1 and themselves. This layer uses prime
factorization as a foundation for encryption algorithms, which are
difficult to reverse-engineer due to the computational complexity
involved in factorizing large numbers.

**Mathematical Integration:** The core engine formula incorporates prime
numbers as multiplicative factors in encryption keys. The difficulty of
prime factorization ensures that even if a key is intercepted, without
the knowledge of the prime factors used, decrypting the data is nearly
impossible. This layer works by encoding information as a product of
prime numbers, utilizing the difficulty of the reverse operation (prime
factorization) as a basis for security​​.
