**A Summary of** **Primary Factors of Multiplicity in Social Physics**

**INGENUITY AWARDS,** are the most esteemed and sought after awards
available to any and all members. As a Research & Development
Organization we are constantly looking for ways to optimize our
resources as an active member of community so that we can multiply our
productivity and our services to the greater community. One of the ways
we do this is by rewarding our members for their ingenuity and
creativity.\
Exchangeable - Transferable

**DESIGNATION REWARDS** are incurred monthly by members who are
rightfully able and willing to designate assets such as land,
facilities, or intellectual property to be under the stewardship of the
organization and used only for purposes approved by the designating
member and the Board of Directors. Designation credits are rewarded
according to the services and productivity that is a direct result of
the designation and are typically rewarded on a monthly basis however in
certain instances designation rewards may be rewarded on a weekly basis
upon resolution of the board. \
Exchangeable - Transferable

**OPPORTUNITY REWARDS** nothing works if we don't, so our aim is to
continuously have a wide variety of opportunities blended between time
slots and full time positions. It takes people to get things done and
it's to the honor of our hard working volunteers,  associates, 
managers, and elected officials that we recognize and reward them for
their time and effort. Opportunity rewards are most often distributed on
weekly basis and are incurred based on the value of reciprocity provided
by the opportunity. Exchangeable - Transferable

**SHARING REWARDS** are annually rewarded credits that any and all
members can receive by inviting 2 new members. For the first referral a
member will receive an annual 1% credit based on the total credits
incurred by referred account and a .5% credit for the second referred
accounts, for as long as the memberships stay active. In the event that
a referred account is closed the positions of the remaining referrals
will shift up and the next account you referred takes the . Sharing
Rewards are distributed within 120 days from the first of the year. \
Non-Exchangeable

**SPONSORSHIP CREDITS** always represent the value of a monetary gift
and to show our appreciation this organization will always value a gift
beyond what anyone says it\'s worth. We reward sponsorship credits
according to the law of multiplicity as the basis for exchange. 
Sponsorship credits do not incur interest; upon cash out any outstanding
sponsorship credits are reimbursed. Our goal as an organization is to
always keep the greatest value and benefits within our community while
at the same time provide services to the general public. Because the
greatest benefit is being a member and the greatest rewards are the
services provided to our members, we strive to maintain a standard of
contemporary professionalism with a quality and at a convenience that
hasn\'t been seen in over a century. \
Exchangeable, Non-Transferable
