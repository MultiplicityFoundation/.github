Memorandum of Points and Authorities

I. Introduction
This is a consumer debt collection action designated CV (civil), not Small Claims. The Complaint omits
mandatory UTCR 5.180(3) caption/notice, and pleads ownership/merger without documents tying this
account to Plaintiff. Dismissal is proper; alternatively, the parties must arbitrate per the governing
agreement.


II. Background (Brief Facts)
1. Plaintiff filed on ★[date].
2. Service allegedly occurred on ★[date]; Defendant appeared on ★[date].
3. Plaintiff alleges ownership via ★[“successor by merger”/assignment/etc.] without attaching a
Schedule of Accounts/PSA extract/Bill of Sale identifying this account.
4. The governing card agreement (attached) contains a mutual arbitration clause designating ★[AAA/
JAMS] and a small‑claims exception not invoked by filing in CV.


III. Argument


A. Facial Non‑Compliance with UTCR 5.180(3) (Consumer Debt Caption/Notice)
The Complaint omits the required “SUBJECT TO UTCR 5.180(3)” caption and consumer notice for
non‑debt‑buyer consumer actions. These are mandatory pleading elements. Omission is a facial defect
warranting dismissal under ORCP 21 A(8) (failure to state ultimate facts) or, at minimum, striking with leave
to replead within a set deadline.


B. Lack of Standing / Real Party in Interest (ORCP 21 A(8))
Plaintiff alleges ownership via merger/assignment yet provides no document tethering this account to
Plaintiff (e.g., PSA extract, Schedule of Accounts, Bill of Sale/Repurchase). Without competent evidence
of title to this claim, the real party in interest is not established. Dismissal is appropriate; alternatively,
order Plaintiff to produce those documents on a short schedule and stay merits discovery pending
compliance.


C. Alternative: Compel Arbitration and Stay (ORS 36.620; 9 U.S.C. § 4)
Defendant elects arbitration under the attached agreement. The case bears a CV designator and was filed
in the Circuit Court Civil Division, not Small Claims; filing outside the Small Claims Department leaves the
arbitration election intact. The Court should compel arbitration before ★[AAA/JAMS] and stay this action
pending the arbitral award. Plaintiff, as a business party, should advance administrator fees per applicable
consumer rules.


D. Alternative Relief (More Definite Statement; Stay of Discovery)
If dismissal or arbitration is not granted, Defendant requests an order requiring Plaintiff, within ★[14] days,
to file a more definite statement that (1) enumerates each cause of action; (2) states facts supporting each
element; and (3) attaches the operative contract and any chain‑of‑title documents; and to stay discovery
until that filing.


IV. Conclusion
Grant the Motion to Dismiss. Alternatively, compel arbitration and stay. Alternatively, require a more
definite statement and stay discovery.
