[[LOCK id=memointro_heading]]Memorandum of Points and Authorities[[/LOCK]]


[[LOCK id=intro]] I. Introduction
This consumer debt collection action bears a CV case type (General Civil). The Complaint omits mandatory
UTCR 5.180(3) caption/notice and pleads “Successor by Merger” without account‑specific facts or
documents establishing ownership. Dismissal is proper under ORCP 21 A(8). Alternatively, the governing
Cardmember Agreement (attached) requires arbitration; the Court should compel and stay under ORS
36.620 and 9 U.S.C. §§ 3–4. [[/LOCK]]

3. Complaint alleges ownership via merger/assignment but attaches no chain‑of‑title for this account.
4. The operative card agreement contains a mutual arbitration clause designating ★[AAA/JAMS];

[[LOCK id=argument_heading]]III. Argument[[/LOCK]]


[[LOCK id=arg_5180]] A. Facial Non‑Compliance with UTCR 5.180(3)
The initiating pleading must include in its title: “SUBJECT TO UTCR 5.180(3),” with accompanying consumer
notice. The omission is a facial defect warranting dismissal under ORCP 21 A(8), or, at minimum, striking
with leave to replead. Prejudice: the missing designation deprives the Court and Defendant of the
consumer‑case track intended by the rule (screening, classification, and evidentiary expectations),
undermining docket management and statutory protections. [[/LOCK]]


[[LOCK id=arg_standing]] B. Lack of Standing / Real Party in Interest (ORCP 21 A(8); ORCP 26 A)
Plaintiff pleads “Successor by Merger to ★[X]” but alleges no ultimate facts tying this account to Plaintiff
at the time of the alleged merger and no exclusion from prior securitizations. Without a Schedule of

real‑party status is unestablished. Dismiss; alternatively, order production on a short timeline and stay

[[LOCK id=arg_arb]] C. Alternative: Compel Arbitration and Stay (ORS 36.620; FAA, 9 U.S.C. §§ 3–4)
Defendant elects arbitration under the attached agreement. Filing as CV (not Small Claims) defeats

should compel arbitration before ★[AAA/JAMS] and stay this action pending the award. If Plaintiff does
not advance administrator fees as required by consumer rules, Defendant may seek appropriate relief. [[/
