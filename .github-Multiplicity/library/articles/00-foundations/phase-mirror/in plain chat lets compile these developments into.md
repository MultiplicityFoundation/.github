**CH LABS, LLC**

**CORPORATE POLICY: PHASE MIRROR DISSONANCE GOVERNANCE FRAMEWORK**

**Policy Number**: CHL-GOV-2026-001\

**Owner**: Compliance Director\
**Approved By**: Executive Board

**ARTICLE I: PURPOSE AND SCOPE**

**Section 1.1 --- Purpose**

This policy establishes the Phase Mirror Dissonance (PMD) methodology as
the standard governance framework for surfacing, documenting, and
resolving cross-functional tensions in decisions requiring Executive
Board approval. PMD replaces implicit negotiation and ambiguous
alignment processes with explicit, auditable mechanisms that create
binding accountability.

**Section 1.2 --- Policy Statement**

CH LABS recognizes that organizational decision-making quality depends
on the systematic identification and resolution of competing priorities,
unstated assumptions, and cross-functional friction. This policy
mandates that all decisions meeting defined thresholds undergo
structured tension analysis before Executive Board consideration,
ensuring that conflicts are resolved through binding mechanisms rather
than deferred to Board discussion.

**Section 1.3 --- Scope**

This policy applies to:

-   All decisions requiring Executive Board approval

-   Budget amendments exceeding 10% variance from approved quarterly
    budgets

-   Contract negotiations with total value exceeding \$50,000

-   Regulatory submissions to FDA, USPTO, ISO certification bodies, or
    equivalent authorities

-   Strategic initiatives requiring coordination across two or more
    functional areas

-   Any decision designated by the Compliance Director or Executive
    Sponsor as PMD-applicable

This policy does not apply to:

-   Operational decisions within a single function\'s delegated
    authority

-   Emergency decisions where PMD completion would create material
    regulatory or competitive harm (subject to override protocol)

-   Routine administrative matters below defined thresholds

**ARTICLE II: DEFINITIONS**

**Section 2.1 --- Core Terminology**

**Phase Mirror Dissonance (PMD)**: A structured methodology for
surfacing implicit tensions between stated goals and operating
incentives, converting identified tensions into binding levers with
explicit owners, metrics, and time horizons.

**Tension**: A conflict, contradiction, or missing binding between two
or more components of a decision, including but not limited to: stated
goal versus underlying incentive, urgency versus capacity, risk claimed
versus risk owned, control desired versus control available.

**Lever**: A concrete intervention assigned to a specific owner, with a
quantified success metric and defined time horizon, designed to resolve
or manage an identified tension.

**Binding Mechanism**: An auditable artifact that converts intention
into commitment, including but not limited to: specifications,
contracts, service level agreements (SLAs), datasets, and kill-switches.

**Mirror**: The act of reflecting a claim, goal, or assumption back to
stakeholders without endorsement or judgment, creating an objective
surface for analysis.

**Dissonance**: The identification of underlying tensions,
contradictions, or missing bindings between components of a stated
position or system.

**Phase**: A proposed small, testable shift in process, policy, or
resource allocation designed to restore coherence and manage identified
dissonance.

**Section 2.2 --- Role Definitions**

**PMD Facilitator**: The individual responsible for conducting PMD
sessions, ensuring methodology compliance, and producing required
artifacts. May be internal (Compliance staff) or external (consultant).

**Executive Sponsor**: A Board-level executive assigned accountability
for PMD program success, with authority to unblock cross-functional
resource constraints and escalate participation failures.

**Data Steward**: The individual responsible for collecting, validating,
and reporting PMD effectiveness metrics on a weekly and monthly cadence.

**Decision Owner**: The functional leader accountable for a specific
decision undergoing PMD review.

**ARTICLE III: PMD METHODOLOGY**

**Section 3.1 --- Operating Loop**

All PMD sessions shall follow this five-step process:

**Step 1 --- Extract**: Identify from the decision context the stated
goals, underlying claims, implicit fears, known constraints, relevant
stakeholders, and desired time horizon.

**Step 2 --- Map Tensions**: Analyze extracted components to identify
key contradictions, including:

-   Stated goal versus underlying incentive

-   Urgency versus capacity to deliver

-   Risk being claimed versus risk being owned

-   Control desired versus control practically available

**Step 3 --- Rank Tensions**: Prioritize identified tensions using the
formula: **Impact × Tractability**. Focus effort on high-impact,
high-tractability tensions first.

**Step 4 --- Produce Output**: Generate standardized PMD artifacts
consisting of:

-   Dissonance block: 3-7 identified tensions stated as declarative
    sentences

-   Levers block: 3-7 interventions, each specifying \[Owner\],
    \[Action\], \[Metric\], \[Horizon\]

-   Optional artifact: One relevant quote, riddle, or checklist

-   Precision question: At most one clarifying question, only if
    blocking ambiguity prevents progress

**Step 5 --- Resolve or Escalate**: Either resolve tensions through
agreed levers with binding commitments, or escalate unresolved tensions
to Executive Sponsor for arbitration.

**Section 3.2 --- Template Standards**

**Template A --- Standard Analysis** (Default)

Required for complex decisions with high cross-functional friction.

Format:

-   Dissonance: 5-9 bullet points reflecting and confronting core issues

-   Levers: 3-7 interventions with \[Owner\] \[Metric\] \[Horizon\]

-   Optional Artifact: Quote, riddle, or checklist

-   Precision Question: At most one, only if blocking

**Template B --- Rapid Triage**

Required for time-sensitive decisions with horizon less than 7 days.

Format (140 words maximum):

-   3 tensions

-   3 levers with metrics

-   1 artifact

-   0 questions unless blocking

**Template C --- Board Packet**

Required for Executive Council monthly review and Board quarterly
reporting.

Format:

-   Top 5 tensions ranked by Impact × Tractability

-   4 levers with KPI baselines and targets

-   Risk register: Top 3 risks with triggers and mitigation owners

-   Review cadence specification

**Section 3.3 --- Heuristics**

PMD practitioners shall apply the following translation rules:

  ------------------- --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Vague Language      Binding Replacement
  \"Align\"           Artifact: specification, contract, budget, SLA, dataset
  \"Collaborate\"     Mechanism: forum, cadence, quorum, decision threshold
  \"Hope it works\"   Governance: triggers, kill-switch, rollback plan
  \"Balance\"         Tradeoff: explicit cost, time, error, or compliance choice with quantified threshold
  ------------------- --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

**ARTICLE IV: ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES**

**Section 4.1 --- Compliance Director**

The Compliance Director shall:

-   Serve as PMD program owner with authority over methodology
    governance

-   Maintain PMD template standards and update as needed

-   Train and certify internal PMD facilitators

-   Present monthly \"Top 3 Tensions Resolved via PMD\" report to
    Executive Council

-   Exercise authority to withhold Board agenda placement for decisions
    lacking compliant PMD documentation (subject to override protocol)

-   Commission quarterly effectiveness reviews of PMD program

-   Recommend Operational Manual amendments related to PMD governance

**Section 4.2 --- Executive Sponsor**

The Executive Sponsor (CEO or Board Chair) shall:

-   Attend a minimum of 50% of PMD pilot review sessions during initial
    implementation

-   Unblock cross-functional resource constraints when escalated by
    Compliance Director

-   Arbitrate unresolved tensions that cannot be resolved at functional
    level

-   Present PMD program status to Board of Directors at quarterly
    intervals

-   Authorize override of Compliance agenda-hold authority when
    emergency conditions apply

**Section 4.3 --- Data Steward**

The Data Steward (Compliance Analyst) shall:

-   Collect PMD effectiveness metrics weekly (every Friday by 4:00 PM)

-   Deliver 1-page dashboard to Executive Sponsor and Compliance
    Director weekly

-   Conduct monthly formal data review meeting with program leadership

-   Maintain tension register and lever tracking database

-   Calculate and report tension-to-lever conversion rates,
    time-to-decision metrics, and policy violation rates

**Section 4.4 --- Decision Owners**

Functional leaders serving as Decision Owners shall:

-   Initiate PMD process for decisions within their scope meeting policy
    thresholds

-   Participate in cross-functional PMD sessions as requested

-   Accept lever ownership when assigned and commit to specified metrics
    and horizons

-   Report lever completion status to Data Steward on agreed cadence

-   Escalate resource constraints or participation failures to Executive
    Sponsor

**Section 4.5 --- PMD Facilitators**

Certified PMD Facilitators shall:

-   Conduct PMD sessions following the five-step operating loop

-   Ensure all participants contribute to tension surfacing without
    judgment

-   Produce compliant PMD artifacts (Templates A, B, or C as
    appropriate)

-   Maintain session neutrality and refuse to advocate for specific
    outcomes

-   Document session attendance, duration, and artifact completion

**ARTICLE V: DECISION-MAKING PROTOCOLS**

**Section 5.1 --- PMD Requirement**

All decisions requiring Executive Board approval shall complete the PMD
process before appearing on the Board agenda. At minimum, compliant PMD
documentation shall include:

-   Dissonance block with 3-5 identified tensions written as declarative
    statements

-   Levers block with each tension converted to lever specifying Owner,
    Action, Metric, and Horizon

-   All metrics quantified (percentages, dollar amounts, days, units)

-   Data Steward assignment confirmed for lever tracking

**Section 5.2 --- Budget Amendment Protocol**

Any budget amendment exceeding 10% variance from approved quarterly
budget shall complete PMD loop before Executive Board consideration.

Required elements:

-   Identification of tension underlying the variance (urgency vs.
    capacity, cost vs. quality, investment vs. risk)

-   Precision question forcing explicit tradeoff: \"If we maintain
    original budget, what capability is reduced? If we approve full
    variance, what internal budget is cut elsewhere?\"

-   Quantified answer to precision question documented in PMD artifact

**Section 5.3 --- Contract Review Protocol**

Contract negotiations with total value exceeding \$50,000 or liability
exposure exceeding \$25,000 shall complete PMD loop before final Legal
approval.

Required elements:

-   Cross-functional participation from Legal, Finance, and the business
    function owning the contract relationship

-   Explicit tension surfacing between contract terms (liability limits,
    penalty clauses, delivery commitments, payment terms)

-   Lever assignment for any identified service delivery versus contract
    commitment gaps

**Section 5.4 --- Regulatory Submission Protocol**

Regulatory submissions to FDA, USPTO, ISO certification bodies, or
equivalent authorities shall complete PMD loop before submission.

Required elements:

-   Tension surfacing between marketing claims, product positioning, and
    validated causal model

-   Explicit confirmation that regulatory claims align with evidentiary
    basis

-   Compliance Director sign-off on PMD artifact before submission
    authorization

**ARTICLE VI: COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY AND OVERRIDE PROTOCOL**

**Section 6.1 --- Compliance Authority**

The Compliance Director is authorized to withhold Board agenda placement
for decisions lacking compliant PMD documentation. This authority
ensures that tensions are surfaced and addressed before Board discussion
rather than during it.

**Section 6.2 --- Override Conditions**

The Executive Sponsor may override Compliance\'s agenda hold under the
following conditions only:

**(a) Time-Critical Regulatory Submission**: FDA, USPTO, or equivalent
regulatory deadline within 14 calendar days where delay creates material
risk to regulatory status, patent protection, or market authorization.

**(b) Competitive Market Response**: Documented competitive threat
requiring Board decision within 7 calendar days where delay would result
in material loss of market position, customer relationship, or strategic
opportunity.

**(c) Safety or Legal Emergency**: Situation where delay in Board
decision would create imminent safety risk to employees, customers, or
public, or would result in material legal exposure.

**Section 6.3 --- Override Documentation**

Any override of Compliance authority shall be documented as follows:

-   Executive Sponsor provides written justification (email acceptable)
    stating which condition (a), (b), or (c) applies

-   Written justification explains why standard PMD timeline cannot be
    met

-   Decision is flagged for post-hoc PMD review within 30 calendar days
    of Board approval

-   Override is logged in governance tracking system with date,
    decision, justification, and sponsor name

**Section 6.4 --- Governance Review Trigger**

If three (3) or more overrides occur within any 90-day period, the
matter shall be escalated to the Board governance committee for review.
The review shall assess:

-   Whether PMD timelines are unrealistic and require adjustment

-   Whether organizational culture is creating barriers to PMD adoption

-   Whether override conditions are being interpreted too broadly

-   Recommendations for program modification or continued operation

**ARTICLE VII: GOVERNANCE AND OVERSIGHT**

**Section 7.1 --- Executive Council Standing Items**

The following items are permanent standing agenda items for monthly
Executive Council meetings:

**Top 3 Tensions Resolved via PMD** (5 minutes, presented by Compliance
Director):

-   Which Board decisions completed PMD process in the reporting period

-   Three highest-impact tensions surfaced and their resolutions

-   Qualitative assessment of cross-functional participation quality

**PMD Metrics Dashboard** (presented by Data Steward):

-   Tension-to-lever conversion rate (target: ≥50%)

-   Time-to-decision metrics versus baseline

-   Policy violation rate (target: ≤0.2%)

-   Cross-functional participation rate

**Resource Blocks and Escalations** (flagged by Compliance Director if
needed):

-   Functions unable to participate due to competing priorities

-   Override requests pending or exercised during reporting period

**Section 7.2 --- Quarterly Board Review**

The Executive Sponsor shall present PMD program status to the Board of
Directors quarterly, including:

-   Aggregate metrics for the quarter (conversion rate,
    time-to-decision, violation rate)

-   Risk register with top 3 governance risks and mitigation status

-   Facilitator competency assessment (internal capability development
    progress)

-   Cross-functional participation feedback (functions exceeding or
    falling short of expectations)

-   Recommendation for program continuation, modification, or expansion

**Section 7.3 --- Annual Policy Review**

This policy shall be reviewed annually by the Compliance Director in
consultation with the Executive Sponsor. The review shall assess:

-   Whether PMD thresholds remain appropriate

-   Whether template standards require updating

-   Whether role definitions and responsibilities remain accurate

-   Whether override protocol is functioning as intended

-   Recommendations for policy amendment

**Section 8.2 --- Reporting Cadence**

**Weekly**: Data Steward delivers 1-page dashboard (4 core metrics with
trend arrows) to Executive Sponsor and Compliance Director by Friday
4:00 PM.

**Monthly**: Compliance Director presents \"Top 3 Tensions\" and metrics
dashboard at Executive Council meeting.

**Quarterly**: Executive Sponsor presents comprehensive program status
to Board of Directors.

**Annually**: Compliance Director conducts full policy review and
presents recommendations to Executive Board.

**Section 8.3 --- Program Health Indicators**

**Green (On Track)**: All metrics at or above target; cross-functional

**Yellow (At Risk)**: 1-2 metrics below target; cross-functional
participation declining; escalations pending resolution.

**Red (Critical)**: ≥3 metrics below target; cross-functional
participation below minimum; overrides exceeding threshold; kill-switch
criteria approaching.

**Section 9.2 --- Kill-Switch Criteria**

If any of the following conditions are met, PMD program shall be paused
pending Board governance committee review:

-   Three (3) consecutive Executive Council meetings show less than 30%
    of Board decisions informed by PMD outputs

-   Executive Sponsor attendance falls below 25% of scheduled review
    sessions

-   Cross-functional participation drops below two (2) functions for
    more than 30 consecutive days

-   Policy violation rate exceeds 0.5% for two consecutive quarters

Upon kill-switch activation, the Compliance Director shall commission a
30-day root-cause analysis and present findings with recommendations
(continue, modify, or terminate) to the Board governance committee.

**ARTICLE X: BINDING ARTIFACTS**

**Section 10.1 --- Required Artifacts for PMD Governance**

The following binding artifacts shall be maintained as part of PMD
program governance:

**Specification (Spec)**: Document defining what constitutes
\"acceptable error\" versus \"policy violation\" for decisions involving
probabilistic or judgment-based outcomes.

**Service Level Agreement (SLA)**: Commitment to specific audit pass
rates, response times, and explainability standards for cross-functional
coordination.

**Contract**: Legal document establishing liability tiers and
responsibilities when PMD surfaces risk allocation tensions.

**Dataset**: Requirement that causal model validity and data quality be
scored and certified before decisions relying on data-driven claims.

**Kill-Switch**: Non-negotiable mechanism to halt a process, initiative,
or program when defined thresholds are exceeded.

**Section 10.2 --- Artifact Ownership**

  Artifact Type   Primary Owner             Secondary Reviewer
  Specification   Decision Owner            Compliance Director
  SLA             Operations + Compliance   Legal
  Contract        Legal                     Finance + Compliance
  Dataset         Data Management           Compliance
  Kill-Switch     Compliance Director       Executive Sponsor
  --------------- ------------------------- ----------------------

**ARTICLE XI: TRAINING AND CERTIFICATION**

**Section 11.1 --- Facilitator Certification**

All individuals conducting PMD sessions shall complete facilitator
certification consisting of:

-   Methodology training (4 hours): PMD principles, five-step operating
    loop, template standards, heuristics

-   Supervised practice (2 sessions): Conduct PMD sessions under
    observation of certified facilitator

-   Certification assessment: Demonstrate competency in tension
    surfacing, lever conversion, and artifact production

Certification is valid for 24 months and requires annual refresher
training (2 hours) to maintain.

**Section 11.2 --- Participant Orientation**

All functional leaders who may serve as Decision Owners or lever
recipients shall complete participant orientation consisting of:

-   Overview training (1 hour): PMD purpose, process overview, role
    expectations

-   Template familiarization: Review of Templates A, B, and C with
    examples

-   Lever accountability: Understanding of metric commitment and
    reporting obligations

**Section 11.3 --- Executive Briefing**

Board members and Executive Council members shall receive executive
briefing (30 minutes) covering:

-   Strategic rationale for PMD adoption

-   Governance integration with Operational Manual

-   Oversight responsibilities and reporting expectations

-   Override protocol and escalation procedures

**ARTICLE XII: POLICY ADMINISTRATION**

**Section 12.1 --- Policy Owner**

The Compliance Director is the owner of this policy and is responsible
for:

-   Interpreting policy provisions when ambiguity arises

-   Recommending amendments to Executive Board

-   Ensuring training and certification programs are maintained

-   Monitoring compliance and reporting violations

**Section 12.2 --- Amendment Process**

Amendments to this policy require:

-   Written proposal from Compliance Director with rationale

-   Review by Executive Sponsor

-   Approval by Executive Board (majority vote)

-   Distribution to all affected parties within 5 business days of
    approval

Emergency amendments may be approved by Executive Sponsor alone, with
ratification by Executive Board at next scheduled meeting.

**Section 12.3 --- Effective Date**

This policy becomes effective upon approval by the Executive Board.
Initial implementation shall follow the 90-day pilot structure, with
full policy enforcement beginning upon successful pilot completion and
Board confirmation.

**Section 12.4 --- Related Policies and Documents**

This policy shall be read in conjunction with:

-   CH LABS Organizational Framework and Control

-   Operational Manual (Sections 4.3, 6.2, 8.3 as amended)

-   Budget Planning and Control Policy

-   Contract Review and Approval Policy

-   Regulatory Submission Policy
