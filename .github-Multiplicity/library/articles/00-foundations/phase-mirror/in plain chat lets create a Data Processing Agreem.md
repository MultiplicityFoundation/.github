**RECITALS**

**WHEREAS**, Controller has engaged Processor to provide AI governance
services, including the Phase Mirror Oracle platform, consulting, and
related services (the \"Services\");

**WHEREAS**, in the course of providing the Services, Processor will
process Personal Data on behalf of Controller;

**WHEREAS**, Controller is subject to the General Data Protection
Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (\"GDPR\") and related data protection laws;

**WHEREAS**, Controller may use Processor\'s services in connection with
AI systems subject to Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (the \"EU AI Act\");

**WHEREAS**, the parties wish to ensure compliance with applicable data
protection laws and establish the terms under which Personal Data will
be processed;

**NOW, THEREFORE**, the parties agree as follows:

**ARTICLE 1: DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION**

**1.1 Definitions**

For purposes of this DPA:

  ----------------------------------------- ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Term                                      Definition
  **Affiliate**                             Any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a party
  **Applicable Data Protection Law**        GDPR, EU AI Act, and all other applicable laws relating to data protection, privacy, and security
  **Controller Personal Data**              Personal Data processed by Processor on behalf of Controller pursuant to this DPA
  **Data Subject**                          An identified or identifiable natural person as defined in GDPR Article 4(1)
  **Data Subject Request**                  A request from a Data Subject to exercise their rights under Applicable Data Protection Law
  **EEA**                                   European Economic Area
  **EU AI Act**                             Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 of the European Parliament and of the Council on Artificial Intelligence
  **Personal Data**                         Any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person as defined in GDPR Article 4(1)
  **Personal Data Breach**                  A breach of security leading to accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure of, or access to, Personal Data
  **Processing**                            Any operation performed on Personal Data as defined in GDPR Article 4(2)
  **Services Agreement**                    The underlying agreement(s) between the parties governing provision of the Services, including Terms of Service, Statements of Work, and subscription agreements
  **Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)**   The standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission for transfers of Personal Data to third countries
  **Sub-processor**                         Any third party engaged by Processor to process Controller Personal Data
  **Supervisory Authority**                 An independent public authority established by an EU Member State pursuant to GDPR Article 51
  ----------------------------------------- ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

**1.2 Interpretation**

References to \"GDPR Article\" or \"GDPR Annex\" mean the article or
annex of the GDPR. Terms not defined in this DPA have the meanings given
in the GDPR.

**ARTICLE 2: SCOPE AND RELATIONSHIP TO SERVICES AGREEMENT**

**2.1 Scope**

This DPA applies to all Processing of Controller Personal Data by
Processor in connection with the Services.

**2.2 Relationship to Services Agreement**

This DPA forms part of and supplements the Services Agreement. In the
event of conflict between this DPA and the Services Agreement with
respect to Processing of Personal Data, this DPA prevails.

**2.3 Hierarchy of Documents**

In the event of conflict, documents take precedence in the following
order:

1.  Standard Contractual Clauses (if applicable)

2.  This Data Processing Agreement

3.  Services Agreement

4.  Service-specific exhibits

**ARTICLE 3: ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES**

**3.1 Respective Roles**

The parties acknowledge and agree that:

\(a\) Controller is the Controller of Controller Personal Data;

\(b\) Processor is the Processor of Controller Personal Data;

\(c\) Each party shall comply with its obligations under Applicable Data
Protection Law;

\(d\) Controller shall ensure it has all necessary legal bases and
provides all required notices for Processor to process Controller
Personal Data as contemplated by the Services Agreement.

**3.2 Controller Instructions**

\(a\) Processor shall process Controller Personal Data only on
documented instructions from Controller, unless required to do so by EU
or Member State law.

\(b\) The Services Agreement, this DPA, and any subsequent written
instructions constitute Controller\'s complete documented instructions
for Processing.

\(c\) If Processor believes an instruction infringes Applicable Data
Protection Law, Processor shall immediately inform Controller.

\(d\) Controller may issue additional written instructions via:

-   Email to \[processor-instructions\@phasemirror.io\]

-   Through the Platform\'s administrative interface (for Platform
    customers)

-   Written notice pursuant to the Services Agreement

**ARTICLE 4: PROCESSOR OBLIGATIONS**

**4.1 Confidentiality**

\(a\) Processor shall ensure that persons authorized to process
Controller Personal Data:

-   Are subject to binding confidentiality obligations (contractual or
    statutory);

-   Receive appropriate training on data protection;

-   Process Personal Data only as necessary for the Services.

\(b\) Processor maintains a list of personnel with access to Controller
Personal Data, available upon request.

**4.2 Security Measures**

\(a\) Processor shall implement appropriate technical and organizational
measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk,
including as appropriate:

-   Pseudonymization and encryption of Personal Data;

-   Ability to ensure ongoing confidentiality, integrity, availability,
    and resilience of Processing systems;

-   Ability to restore availability and access to Personal Data in a
    timely manner after an incident;

-   Regular testing, assessment, and evaluation of effectiveness of
    security measures.

\(b\) **Annex II attached hereto describes** Processor\'s technical and
organizational security measures.

\(c\) Processor shall take steps to ensure that Sub-processors implement
equivalent security measures.

**4.3 Sub-processing**

\(a\) Controller provides general authorization for Processor to engage
Sub-processors, subject to the requirements of this Article 4.3.

\(b\) **Current Sub-processors** are listed in **Annex III attached
hereto**.

\(c\) **New Sub-processors:** Processor shall inform Controller at least
**30 days** in advance of any intended addition or replacement of
Sub-processors. Controller may object to such changes on reasonable
grounds relating to data protection within **14 days** of notification.

\(d\) If Controller objects:

-   Parties shall discuss in good faith to address concerns;

-   If unresolved, Controller may terminate the affected Services
    without penalty;

-   Processor shall not engage the objected Sub-processor for
    Controller\'s data.

\(e\) Processor shall:

-   Impose data protection obligations on Sub-processors that are
    equivalent to those in this DPA;

-   Ensure Sub-processors comply with GDPR Chapter V for international
    transfers;

-   Remain fully liable to Controller for Sub-processor performance.

\(f\) Upon Controller request, Processor shall provide copies of
Sub-processor agreements (with commercially sensitive terms redacted).

**4.4 Data Subject Rights**

\(a\) Taking into account the nature of Processing, Processor shall
assist Controller by implementing appropriate technical and
organizational measures, insofar as possible, to fulfill Controller\'s
obligations to respond to Data Subject Requests.

\(b\) **Requests Received by Processor:**\
If Processor receives a Data Subject Request directly, Processor shall:

-   Forward the request to Controller within **2 business days**;

-   Not respond to the request except on Controller\'s documented
    instructions or as required by law;

-   Inform the Data Subject that Processor is acting as Processor and
    refer them to Controller.

\(c\) **Assistance to Controller:**\
Upon Controller\'s request, Processor shall assist Controller in
responding to Data Subject Requests by:

  ---------------------------- -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Right (GDPR Article)         Processor Assistance
  Access (Art. 15)             Provide access to Controller Personal Data in Processor\'s systems within 10 business days
  Rectification (Art. 16)      Rectify inaccurate Personal Data as directed by Controller within 5 business days
  Erasure (Art. 17)            Delete Personal Data as directed by Controller within 10 business days
  Restriction (Art. 18)        Restrict Processing as directed by Controller within 5 business days
  Data Portability (Art. 20)   Provide Personal Data in structured, commonly used, machine-readable format within 10 business days
  Objection (Art. 21)          Cease Processing as directed by Controller within 5 business days
  ---------------------------- -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

\(d\) Processor may charge reasonable fees for assistance beyond
ordinary obligations, calculated at \[rate\] per hour.

**4.5 Data Protection Impact Assessment and Prior Consultation**

\(a\) Processor shall provide reasonable assistance to Controller in
conducting Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIAs) under GDPR Article
35, if required.

\(b\) Processor shall provide reasonable assistance to Controller in
consulting with Supervisory Authorities under GDPR Article 36, if
required.

\(c\) Such assistance includes:

-   Description of Processing operations and purposes;

-   Assessment of necessity and proportionality of Processing;

-   Description of security measures implemented;

-   Information about Sub-processors and international transfers.

**4.6 Personal Data Breach Notification**

\(a\) Processor shall notify Controller without undue delay and in any
event within **24 hours** of becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach
affecting Controller Personal Data.

\(b\) Notification shall include:

-   Description of the nature of the breach, including where possible:

    -   Categories and approximate number of Data Subjects affected;

    -   Categories and approximate number of Personal Data records
        affected;

-   Name and contact details of Processor\'s data protection officer or
    other contact point;

-   Description of likely consequences of the breach;

-   Description of measures taken or proposed to address the breach and
    mitigate its possible adverse effects.

\(c\) If information cannot be provided simultaneously, Processor shall
provide information in phases without undue further delay.

\(d\) Processor shall:

-   Document all Personal Data Breaches;

-   Cooperate with Controller\'s breach investigation;

-   Provide Controller with information necessary for Controller to
    notify Supervisory Authorities and Data Subjects;

-   Not notify Supervisory Authorities, Data Subjects, or third parties
    without Controller\'s prior written consent, except as required by
    law.

\(e\) **Breach Investigation and Remediation:**\
Processor shall:

-   Conduct root cause analysis within 10 business days;

-   Implement remediation measures within timeframe agreed with
    Controller;

-   Provide written breach report to Controller within 15 business days;

-   Reimburse Controller for reasonable costs of mandatory Data Subject
    notifications caused by Processor\'s breach (subject to limitations
    in Services Agreement).

**4.7 Deletion or Return of Personal Data**

\(a\) Upon termination or expiration of the Services Agreement,
Processor shall, at Controller\'s choice:

-   Delete all Controller Personal Data; or

-   Return all Controller Personal Data to Controller.

\(b\) **Timeline:** Within **30 days** of termination/expiration.

\(c\) **Certification:** Processor shall certify in writing to
Controller that it has complied with this Article 4.7.

\(d\) **Exceptions:** Processor may retain Controller Personal Data to
the extent required by applicable law, provided that Processor shall:

-   Inform Controller of such legal requirement before retention;

-   Ensure confidentiality and limit Processing to purposes required by
    law;

-   Continue to protect Personal Data in accordance with this DPA;

-   Delete when retention is no longer legally required.

\(e\) **Backups:** Personal Data in backup systems will be deleted in
accordance with Processor\'s standard backup deletion cycle (maximum 90
days) following active deletion.

**4.8 Audits and Inspections**

\(a\) Processor shall make available to Controller all information
necessary to demonstrate compliance with GDPR Article 28.

\(b\) **Documentation:** Upon reasonable request, Processor shall
provide:

-   Current security policies and procedures;

-   SOC 2 Type II report (under NDA);

-   Evidence of Sub-processor compliance;

-   Personnel training records;

-   Incident logs.

\(c\) **Audits:** Processor shall allow for and contribute to audits,
including inspections, conducted by Controller or an auditor mandated by
Controller, subject to:

  --------------------- -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Audit Element         Terms
  **Frequency**         Once per year, plus additional audits if reasonable grounds exist
  **Notice**            30 days\' advance written notice (except for urgent breach investigations)
  **Scope**             Limited to Controller Personal Data and compliance with this DPA
  **Timing**            During Processor\'s business hours
  **Confidentiality**   Auditor must execute Processor\'s standard NDA
  **Cost**              Controller bears cost; Processor may charge for time beyond 8 hours at \[rate\]/hour
  **Disruption**        Audits conducted to minimize disruption to Processor\'s business
  **Report**            Controller provides audit report within 30 days; parties address findings in good faith
  --------------------- -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

\(d\) Controller may rely on third-party certifications (SOC 2 Type II,
ISO 27001) in lieu of on-site audit, provided such certifications are
current (within 12 months).

**4.9 Records of Processing Activities**

Processor shall maintain records of Processing activities carried out on
behalf of Controller in accordance with GDPR Article 30(2), including:

-   Name and contact details of Processor and Controller;

-   Categories of Processing;

-   Description of technical and organizational security measures;

-   Where applicable, transfers to third countries and appropriate
    safeguards.

Processor shall make such records available to Supervisory Authorities
upon request.

**5.6 Government Access Requests**

-   Notify Controller immediately unless legally prohibited;

    it is unlawful;

-   Provide minimum Personal Data necessary to comply;

-   Document the request and response;

-   Inform Controller of legal prohibition if notification was initially
    prohibited but prohibition is later lifted.

\(b\) Processor publishes an annual transparency report detailing
government requests received (in aggregate, anonymized form).

**ARTICLE 6: EU AI ACT COMPLIANCE**

**6.1 Applicability**

This Article applies when Controller uses Processor\'s Services in
connection with AI systems subject to the EU AI Act.

**6.2 Nature of AI-Related Processing**

Controller acknowledges that Processor provides:

-   AI governance analysis tools (Phase Mirror Oracle);

-   Risk classification engines for EU AI Act compliance;

-   Documentation and conformity assessment support;

-   Consulting services for AI governance.

Processor does not:

-   Develop or deploy AI systems on Controller\'s behalf;

-   Make decisions about AI system design or use cases;

-   Determine purposes or means of AI system deployment.

**6.3 Processing for AI Governance Purposes**

\(a\) **Personal Data in AI System Documentation:**\
Controller may provide Personal Data to Processor as part of:

-   AI system technical documentation;

-   Risk assessments and logs;

-   Testing and validation datasets;

-   Incident reports and monitoring data.

\(b\) **Purpose Limitation:**\
Processor shall process such Personal Data solely to provide AI
governance services to Controller, not for:

-   Training Processor\'s own AI systems (unless explicitly agreed and
    anonymized);

-   Benchmarking Controller\'s AI systems publicly;

-   Any purpose beyond the Services.

\(c\) **Special Categories of Personal Data:**\
If Controller\'s AI system processes special categories of Personal Data
(GDPR Article 9) or Personal Data relating to criminal convictions (GDPR
Article 10):

-   Controller shall notify Processor in writing;

-   Additional security measures specified in Annex II shall apply;

-   Controller warrants it has legal basis under GDPR Article 9(2) or
    national law.

**6.4 High-Risk AI Systems**

\(a\) If Controller uses Services for AI systems classified as
\"high-risk\" under EU AI Act Article 6 and Annex III:

**Enhanced Record-Keeping:**

-   Processor shall retain records of Processing activities for minimum
    **10 years** from completion of Services (per EU AI Act Article 12);

-   Records shall include: date and time of Processing, purpose, data
    categories, recipients, and retention periods;

-   Controller may request copies of such records for regulatory
    purposes.

**Enhanced Security:**

-   Processor shall implement additional security measures appropriate
    for high-risk systems;

-   Annual third-party security audit (ISO 27001 or equivalent);

-   Enhanced logging and monitoring.

**Support for Conformity Assessment:**

-   Processor shall provide information necessary for Controller\'s
    conformity assessment under EU AI Act Article 43;

-   Processor shall cooperate with notified bodies conducting
    Controller\'s conformity assessment;

-   Processor may charge reasonable fees for extensive conformity
    support beyond ordinary obligations.

\(b\) **Controller Notification Obligation:**\
Controller shall notify Processor in writing if an AI system for which
Services are used is classified as high-risk. Notification triggers
enhanced obligations above.

**6.5 Prohibited AI Practices**

\(a\) Controller warrants that it will not use Processor\'s Services for
AI practices prohibited under EU AI Act Article 5, including:

-   Social scoring systems;

-   Subliminal manipulation techniques;

-   Exploitation of vulnerabilities;

-   Real-time remote biometric identification in publicly accessible
    spaces (except as permitted).

\(b\) If Processor becomes aware that Services are being used for
prohibited practices, Processor may:

-   Suspend Services immediately;

-   Notify relevant Supervisory Authority;

-   Terminate Services Agreement without penalty.

**6.6 Transparency Obligations**

\(a\) Processor shall maintain transparency regarding its Processing of
Personal Data for AI governance purposes, including:

-   Clear documentation of how Personal Data is used in dissonance
    reports;

-   Explanation of risk classification methodology;

-   Description of automated decision-making (if any) in governance
    tools.

\(b\) Processor shall provide Controller with information necessary for
Controller to fulfill its transparency obligations under EU AI Act
Article 13.

**6.7 Incident Reporting for AI Systems**

\(a\) If Processor becomes aware of:

-   A serious incident involving Controller\'s AI system (as defined in
    EU AI Act Article 73);

-   A malfunction or breach that could lead to serious incident;

-   Personal Data Breach related to AI system;

Processor shall notify Controller within **24 hours** with sufficient
detail for Controller to assess reporting obligations to market
surveillance authorities.

\(b\) Processor shall cooperate with any investigation by market
surveillance authorities related to Controller\'s AI systems.

**6.8 Human Oversight**

\(a\) Processor\'s Services are designed to support, not replace, human
decision-making in AI governance.

\(b\) All dissonance reports, risk classifications, and governance
recommendations generated by Processor\'s tools:

-   Are advisory in nature;

-   Require human review and approval before implementation;

-   Do not constitute binding determinations or legal advice.

\(c\) Controller retains full responsibility for decisions regarding AI
system design, deployment, and governance.

**ARTICLE 7: LIABILITY AND INDEMNIFICATION**

**7.1 Liability Under GDPR**

\(a\) Each party\'s liability under GDPR Article 82 shall be subject to
the limitations in the Services Agreement, except where such limitations
are prohibited by law.

\(b\) Processor shall be liable for damages caused by Processing only
where it:

-   Has not complied with obligations specifically directed to
    Processors under GDPR; or

-   Has acted outside or contrary to lawful instructions of Controller.

\(c\) Processor shall not be liable where it proves the event giving
rise to damage is not in any way attributable to Processor.

**7.2 Chain of Liability**

Where both Controller and Processor are involved in the same Processing
and liable for damages:

-   Each shall be held liable for the entire damage to ensure effective
    compensation;

-   Processor may seek recourse from Sub-processors for the portion of
    damages attributable to Sub-processor;

-   Where Processor has paid full compensation, Processor is entitled to
    claim back from Controller or another Processor involved the portion
    corresponding to their part of responsibility.

**7.3 Indemnification**

\(a\) **Processor Indemnification:**\
Processor shall indemnify Controller against:

-   GDPR fines and penalties imposed on Controller due to Processor\'s
    breach of this DPA;

-   Reasonable costs of Data Subject compensation claims caused by
    Processor\'s breach;

-   Reasonable legal fees incurred by Controller defending claims caused
    by Processor\'s breach.

\(b\) **Controller Indemnification:**\
Controller shall indemnify Processor against:

-   Claims arising from Controller\'s unlawful instructions;

-   Claims arising from Controller\'s failure to obtain valid legal
    basis for Processing;

-   GDPR penalties imposed on Processor for following Controller\'s
    documented instructions despite Processor\'s notice that
    instructions violate GDPR.

\(c\) **Indemnification Process:**

-   Indemnified party shall notify indemnifying party promptly of claim;

-   Indemnifying party has right to control defense;

-   Indemnified party shall cooperate in defense;

-   No settlement without indemnified party\'s consent if settlement
    imposes obligations on indemnified party.

\(d\) **Caps:**\
Indemnification obligations are subject to liability caps in Services
Agreement, except where prohibited by law.

**ARTICLE 8: TERM AND TERMINATION**

duration of the Services Agreement or until all Controller Personal Data
is deleted or returned, whichever is later.

**8.2 Termination for Cause**

-   Processor commits material breach and fails to remedy within 30
    days;

-   Processor is subject to insolvency proceedings;

-   Processor cannot provide adequate safeguards for international
    transfers.

\(b\) Upon such termination:

-   Services Agreement also terminates (or relevant Services are
    removed);

-   Processor shall delete or return Personal Data per Article 4.7;

-   Controller is not liable for fees for terminated Services.

**8.3 Survival**

The following provisions survive termination:

-   Article 4.7 (Deletion or Return of Personal Data);

-   Article 4.8 (Audits for prior period);

-   Article 7 (Liability and Indemnification);

-   Article 9.4 (Governing Law);

-   Any other provisions that by their nature should survive.
