**PHASE MIRROR VENDOR ONBOARDING PLAYBOOK**

**A Step-by-Step Guide to Successful Implementation**

**Version:** 2.0 \| **Date:** January 2026\
**Document Owner:** Customer Success Team\
**Classification:** Customer Confidential

**WELCOME TO PHASE MIRROR**

**About This Playbook**

This playbook guides your organization through the complete process of
onboarding Phase Mirror as a vendor, from initial procurement through
full production deployment. Whether you\'re a regulated financial
institution with rigorous third-party risk management requirements or a
technology company implementing your first AI governance solution, this
guide provides the structure you need for success.

**What This Playbook Covers:**

  ---------------------------------------- ----------- ------------------------------------------
  Phase                                    Duration    Key Activities
  **Phase 0:** Pre-Engagement              1-2 weeks   Stakeholder alignment, business case
  **Phase 1:** Procurement & Contracting   2-4 weeks   Due diligence, contract negotiation
  **Phase 2:** Technical Setup             1-2 weeks   Environment provisioning, integrations
  **Phase 3:** Configuration               2-4 weeks   AI system registration, governance setup
  **Phase 4:** Pilot & Validation          2-4 weeks   Testing, validation, refinement
  **Phase 5:** Production Rollout          2-4 weeks   Go-live, training, adoption
  **Phase 6:** Steady State                Ongoing     Monitoring, optimization, expansion
  ---------------------------------------- ----------- ------------------------------------------

**Total Timeline:** 10-20 weeks (depending on complexity and tier)
