**SLIDE 4: THE PHASE MIRROR SOLUTION**

**Surface Contradictions. Create Levers. Govern What Acts.**

**Phase Mirror is the only platform that:**

1.  **Surfaces productive contradictions** between stated governance and
    actual AI behavior

2.  **Converts those tensions into actionable levers** with owners,
    metrics, and timelines

3.  **Creates binding artifacts** (specs, contracts, SLAs) instead of
    vague policies

**Our Methodology: Mirror → Dissonance → Phase**

  ---------------- ------------------------------------------------- --------------------------
  Step             What It Means                                     Output
  **Mirror**       Reflect your claims back without endorsement      Neutral factual baseline
  **Dissonance**   Identify underlying tensions and contradictions   Root problems exposed
  **Phase**        Propose small, testable, incremental shifts       Concrete action levers
  ---------------- ------------------------------------------------- --------------------------

**Example Dissonance:**

-   **Your Policy:** \"All AI decisions require human oversight\"

-   **Your Reality:** Fraud detection AI approves 10,000
    transactions/hour autonomously

-   **Our Lever:** \"Define \'oversight\' as human-reviewable audit
    trail + exception escalation\"
