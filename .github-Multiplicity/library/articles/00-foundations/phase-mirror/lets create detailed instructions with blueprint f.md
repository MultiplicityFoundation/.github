**Phase Mirror Website Blueprint & Detailed Instructions**

**Executive Summary**

The Phase Mirror website serves as the digital front door for a
diagnostic methodology that converts AI governance contradictions into
actionable mechanisms. The site must balance three audiences: **C-suite
executives seeking governance frameworks**, **technical teams
implementing controls**, and **governance practitioners building
expertise**. This blueprint prioritizes clarity over cleverness,
mechanisms over vibes, and conversion through education.

**1. Strategic Positioning & Core Messaging**

**Value Proposition Hierarchy**

**Primary Headline (Hero Section):**\
\"AI Governance by Design, Not by Hope\"

**Supporting Subheadline:**\
\"Phase Mirror converts productive contradictions in AI autonomy into
binding governance mechanisms---replacing vague alignment goals with
auditable levers.\"

**Core Differentiation:**\
Unlike GRC platforms that offer compliance checklists, Phase Mirror
addresses the **structural friction** between AI autonomy and enterprise
governance through a diagnostic methodology that surfaces dissonances
before they become liabilities.

**Three-Pillar Messaging Framework**

1.  **For Executives:** \"Govern agentic AI as a fiduciary duty, not an
    IT project\"

2.  **For Technical Teams:** \"Auditable inconsistency detection built
    into your CI/CD pipeline\"

3.  **For Governance Practitioners:** \"Methodology + Platform +
    Certification ecosystem\"
