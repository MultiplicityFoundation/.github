**Appendix B: When in Doubt, Execute the BAA**

**It\'s safer to have a BAA than to risk a HIPAA violation.**

**Red flags that mean \"execute the BAA\":**

-   Anyone on your team says \"I think there might be PHI\"

-   You\'re unsure about de-identification

-   Your legal team hasn\'t explicitly approved de-identification

-   You\'re in healthcare and analyzing healthcare AI systems

-   You can\'t definitively answer \"No\" to all PHI presence questions

**Remember:** A BAA protects both parties. There\'s no downside to
having one in place.
