                    Comprehensive Services Catalog
Overview

The Phase Mirror services portfolio addresses the full lifecycle of AI governance
adoption—from initial strategic assessment through ongoing operational support. The service
architecture follows a "land and expand" model: organizations typically enter through
consulting engagements or the free platform tier, then expand into enterprise seats, support
contracts, and certification programs as governance maturity increases.

I. Strategic Consulting Services

Strategic consulting represents the highest-margin service tier, targeting C-suite executives,
board members, and senior governance leaders. These engagements leverage the Phase
Mirror methodology to diagnose organizational tensions around AI adoption and produce
binding governance artifacts.[1][2][3]


A. AI Governance Diagnostic (Flagship Engagement)

Purpose: Surface the productive contradictions between AI autonomy and enterprise
governance requirements, converting abstract concerns into concrete, actionable levers.[4][1]

Methodology: Full Phase Mirror Dissonance analysis—Extract → Map Tensions → Rank →
Produce Output → Precision Question[2]

B. Compliance-Accuracy Tradeoff Workshop

Purpose: Force the critical policy decision that the Phase Mirror methodology demands: Does
the agent optimize for the most accurate outcome or the most compliant one?[5][1][2]

This is not a technical configuration—it is the defining governance choice that dictates liability
architecture. The workshop ensures this tradeoff is named, debated, and codified into explicit
policy.[5]

Format: 2-day facilitated executive workshop

Summary: Service Architecture

The Phase Mirror services portfolio creates multiple revenue streams across the customer
lifecycle, with each service reinforcing platform adoption and governance methodology
entrenchment:

  1.​ Consulting establishes relationships and diagnoses governance gaps

  2.​ Implementation deploys the Oracle and creates operational dependency

  3.​ Platform provides ongoing value and usage-based expansion

  4.​ Enterprise Seats bundle compliance content for regulated industries

  5.​ Support Contracts ensure retention through guaranteed service levels

  6.​ Certification creates ecosystem lock-in and quality assurance

  7.​ Training develops practitioner community and drives adoption

This architecture positions Phase Mirror as the operating system for AI governance—not merely
a tool, but a methodology, platform, and ecosystem that organizations adopt comprehensively
rather than piecemeal.​
