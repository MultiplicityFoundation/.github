**Phase Mirror: Comprehensive Services Catalog**

**Overview**

The Phase Mirror services portfolio addresses the full lifecycle of AI
governance adoption---from initial strategic assessment through ongoing
operational support. The service architecture follows a \"land and
expand\" model: organizations typically enter through consulting
engagements or the free platform tier, then expand into enterprise
seats, support contracts, and certification programs as governance
maturity increases.

  ------------------------- -------------------------- --------------------------------- -----------------------
  Service Category          Delivery Model             Target Buyer                      Revenue Type
  Strategic Consulting      Project-based / Retainer   C-Suite, Board                    High-margin services
  Implementation Services   Fixed-price / T&M          IT Leadership, Governance Teams   Professional services
  Platform (SaaS)           Subscription               Engineering, DevOps, Compliance   Recurring ARR
  Enterprise Seats          Annual license             Procurement, IT                   Recurring ARR
  Support Contracts         Annual SLA                 IT Operations                     Recurring ARR
  Certification             Per-certification fee      Individuals, Vendors              Transaction + Renewal
  Training                  Per-seat / Corporate       L&D, HR, Compliance               Blended
  ------------------------- -------------------------- --------------------------------- -----------------------

**I. Strategic Consulting Services**

Strategic consulting represents the highest-margin service tier,
targeting C-suite executives, board members, and senior governance
leaders. These engagements leverage the Phase Mirror methodology to
diagnose organizational tensions around AI adoption and produce binding
governance
artifacts.[^[\[1\]]{.underline}^](#fn1)[^[\[2\]]{.underline}^](#fn2)[^[\[3\]]{.underline}^](#fn3)

**A. AI Governance Diagnostic (Flagship Engagement)**

**Purpose**: Surface the productive contradictions between AI autonomy
and enterprise governance requirements, converting abstract concerns
into concrete, actionable
levers.[^[\[4\]]{.underline}^](#fn4)[^[\[1\]]{.underline}^](#fn1)

**Methodology**: Full Phase Mirror Dissonance analysis---Extract → Map
Tensions → Rank → Produce Output → Precision
Question[^[\[2\]]{.underline}^](#fn2)

  ---------------------- ------------------------------------------------------------ ----------
  Engagement Element     Deliverable                                                  Timeline
  Executive Discovery    Stakeholder interviews (5-8), document review                Week 1-2
  Tension Mapping        Ranked dissonance matrix with impact × tractability scores   Week 2-3
  Lever Development      10-15 actionable levers with owners, metrics, horizons       Week 3-4
  Governance Artifacts   Spec, SLA templates, kill-switch protocols                   Week 4-5
  Board Presentation     Template C Board Packet output with risk register            Week 5-6
  ---------------------- ------------------------------------------------------------ ----------

**Pricing Model**: Fixed-fee based on organizational complexity

  --------------------------------------- ---------------------- -------------
  Organization Size                       Engagement Fee         Duration
  Mid-Market (500-2,000 employees)        \$75,000--\$125,000    6 weeks
  Enterprise (2,000-10,000 employees)     \$150,000--\$250,000   8-10 weeks
  Global Enterprise (10,000+ employees)   \$300,000--\$500,000   12-16 weeks
  --------------------------------------- ---------------------- -------------

**B. Compliance-Accuracy Tradeoff Workshop**

**Purpose**: Force the critical policy decision that the Phase Mirror
methodology demands: *Does the agent optimize for the most accurate
outcome or the most compliant
one?*[^[\[5\]]{.underline}^](#fn5)[^[\[1\]]{.underline}^](#fn1)[^[\[2\]]{.underline}^](#fn2)

This is not a technical configuration---it is the defining governance
choice that dictates liability architecture. The workshop ensures this
tradeoff is named, debated, and codified into explicit
policy.[^[\[5\]]{.underline}^](#fn5)

**Format**: 2-day facilitated executive workshop

  ------- ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- -----------------------------------------
  Day     Focus                                                                           Outputs
  Day 1   Map existing AI systems, surface liability exposures, identify tension points   AI system inventory, risk heat map
  Day 2   Define error budgets, escalation protocols, compliance override triggers        Policy document, exception handler spec
  ------- ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- -----------------------------------------

**Pricing**: \$35,000--\$50,000 (includes pre-work, facilitation,
deliverables)

**C. Advisory Retainer (Pay for Access)**

**Purpose**: Ongoing strategic guidance for organizations navigating AI
governance decisions. Retainer clients receive priority access to senior
Phase Mirror practitioners for ad-hoc
counsel.[^[\[6\]]{.underline}^](#fn6)[^[\[7\]]{.underline}^](#fn7)

**Model**: Monthly retainer for guaranteed access, not hours worked

  ----------------------- ---------------- --------------------------------------------- ---------------
  Tier                    Monthly Fee      Access Level                                  Response Time
  **Advisor**             \$10,000/month   4 hours/month advisory calls, email access    24-48 hours
  **Strategic Partner**   \$25,000/month   Unlimited advisory calls, quarterly on-site   4-8 hours
  **Board Advisor**       \$50,000/month   Board meeting attendance, crisis response     2 hours
  ----------------------- ---------------- --------------------------------------------- ---------------

**Contract Terms**: 6-month minimum commitment, 90-day termination
notice[^[\[8\]]{.underline}^](#fn8)[^[\[6\]]{.underline}^](#fn6)

**Value Positioning**: Apply the 5X Rule---a \$25,000/month retainer
should deliver \$125,000/month in value through risk mitigation,
liability reduction, and accelerated
decision-making.[^[\[6\]]{.underline}^](#fn6)

**II. Implementation Services**

Implementation services translate governance strategy into operational
reality. These engagements deploy the Phase Mirror Oracle, integrate
with existing toolchains, and establish ongoing governance
workflows.[^[\[9\]]{.underline}^](#fn9)[^[\[10\]]{.underline}^](#fn10)[^[\[2\]]{.underline}^](#fn2)

**A. Phase Mirror Platform Implementation**

**Scope**: Deploy Phase Mirror Oracle in customer environment, configure
rules, integrate with CI/CD pipelines

initiation, 40% midpoint, 30%

**B. Governance Framework Development**

**Purpose**: Create the binding artifacts that translate Phase Mirror
methodology into enforceable
governance[^[\[12\]]{.underline}^](#fn12)[^[\[2\]]{.underline}^](#fn2)

**Deliverables**:

-   **Spec Document**: Defines acceptable error vs. policy violation
    thresholds

-   **Liability Contracts**: Establishes tiers between organization and
    AI platform providers

-   **SLAs**: Commits to audit pass rates and explainability standards

-   **Kill-Switch Protocols**: Governance triggers and rollback
    mechanisms

-   **Dataset Requirements**: Causal model validity scoring before
    production[^[\[2\]]{.underline}^](#fn2)

**Pricing**: \$50,000--\$150,000 depending on organizational complexity

**X. Alignment with Phase Mirror License v1.0**

All services operate within the licensing framework established by the
Phase Mirror License v1.0:[^[\[13\]]{.underline}^](#fn13)

  -------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------------------------
  Service                          License Alignment
  **Platform (Self-Hosted)**       Permitted under base license
  **Platform (Managed Service)**   Proof Labs exclusive; requires commercial agreement for others
  **Consulting**                   Permitted; consultants implement for clients who operate
  **Compliance Packs**             Proprietary; separate license
  **Certification**                Trademark-based; separate agreement
  **Training**                     Content proprietary; platform access per license
  -------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------------------------

The license correctly protects managed service revenue while enabling
broad adoption through permitted self-hosting, modification, and
redistribution.[^[\[13\]]{.underline}^](#fn13)

**Summary: Service Architecture**

The Phase Mirror services portfolio creates multiple revenue streams
across the customer lifecycle, with each service reinforcing platform
adoption and governance methodology entrenchment:

1.  **Consulting** establishes relationships and diagnoses governance
    gaps

2.  **Implementation** deploys the Oracle and creates operational
    dependency

3.  **Platform** provides ongoing value and usage-based expansion

4.  **Enterprise Seats** bundle compliance content for regulated
    industries

5.  **Support Contracts** ensure retention through guaranteed service
    levels

6.  **Certification** creates ecosystem lock-in and quality assurance

7.  **Training** develops practitioner community and drives adoption

This architecture positions Phase Mirror as the operating system for AI
governance---not merely a tool, but a methodology, platform, and
ecosystem that organizations adopt comprehensively rather than
piecemeal.
