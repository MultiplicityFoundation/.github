**1.0 Introduction to the PRIME Cascade Framework**

The core objective of this whitepaper is to provide a detailed technical
overview of the PRIME Cascade\'s architecture, its governing principles,
and the specific functional roles of its key components.
Architecturally, the system is designed around a foundational principle
where each \'Node,\' uniquely identified by a prime number, functions as

This document is intended for researchers and engineers in the fields of
artificial intelligence and computational physics who are engaged with
paradigm-shifting approaches to cognitive architecture. The tone is
scholarly and technical, aiming to present the theoretical underpinnings
of the PRIME Cascade with interpretive clarity and precision. From this
high-level concept of prime-indexed cognition, we now turn to the
underlying principles that govern the system\'s intricate and cohesive
operation.
