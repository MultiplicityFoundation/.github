                                    Abstract
         This paper presents a conceptual cosmology model — Nested Equation
     Cosmology (NEC) — derived from neurodiverse pattern-recognition in-
     sights and aligned with scale-invariant physics. NEC rejects a singularity-
     origin universe (Big Bang) in favor of an infinite, self-similar process in
     which structures emerge from recursive geometric relationships (“equation
     in equation”) at all scales.
         The model introduces two novel physical reinterpretations: (1) Re-
     placement of “dark matter” with an unseen, pervasive substance whose
     effects arise from the fluid connectivity of quantum entanglement;
     (2) Recognition that all-directional subatomic flow, mediated by gluon-
     quark interactions, forms an entangled web across the universe, dynami-
     cally independent from standard mass equations.


2    Introduction
Standard ΛCDM cosmology postulates a finite-age universe initiated by a hot,
dense singularity, with subsequent inflation, matter formation, and structure
growth driven by cold dark matter and dark energy.
    NEC challenges this model by proposing that both the geometry and the
underlying substance of the universe are continuous and self-similar at all
scales, and that apparent anomalies in galactic rotation, lensing, and structure
growth do not require exotic cold dark matter particles, but rather emerge from
a quantum-entangled substrate.


3    Core Premise: Equation in Equation ()
The NEC model rests on three pillars:

    • Continuous Connectivity: No true “empty space” exists; all regions

4     Quantum Fluid Connectivity as the Dark Mat-
      ter Replacement
4.1    Unseen Substance
The gravitational anomalies attributed to dark matter are reframed as mani-
festations of an invisible, continuous quantum fluid permeating all space.
This medium is not particulate in the WIMP/axion sense, but rather a field
of entangled relationships whose connectivity produces additional gravitational
effects.

4.2    All-Directional Flow at the Subatomic Level
At subatomic scales, quarks interact through gluon exchange in quantum chro-
modynamics (QCD). NEC extends this by positing that these interactions gen-
erate net all-directional flows — the cumulative effect of which forms a
dynamic tension network throughout the universe.

4.3    Multiplication and Decomposition
    • Multiplication: Gluon-mediated interactions can effectively “multiply”
      quark presence within the quantum fluid without creating rest mass, am-
      plifying gravitational influence without additional baryonic matter.

    • Decomposition: Entangled quark states can break down, redistribut-
      ing connection pathways in the universal network, subtly altering local
      gravitational potentials without mass exchange.


5     Observational Anchors
5.1    Lensing Tests
Multi-band gravitational lensing datasets (optical, CMB, SZ) show no significant
Yukawa-type deviation from GR at cluster scales. NEC interprets this as local-
scale stability in the quantum fluid’s gravitational influence, compatible with
GR’s local geometry.

5.3    Flow Patterns Across Scales
From weather systems to galaxy rotation curves, flow geometries match recursive
mathematical forms, supporting the quantum fluid connectivity model.


6     Relationship to Existing Models
6.1    Overlaps
    • Fractal cosmology — structural self-similarity at large scales.
    • Scale relativity — invariance of laws under scale transformations.

    • Modified gravity models — addressing rotation curve anomalies without
      particulate dark matter.

6.2    Divergences
    • NEC does not require a discrete cosmic start or end.

    • NEC replaces particulate dark matter with an entangled quantum fluid.
    • NEC integrates cognitive pattern recognition as a legitimate cosmological
      tool.


7     Predictions

    • Deep lensing surveys will detect gravitational anomalies that correlate

    • CMB and large-scale structure surveys should reveal fractal dimension
      stability across unprecedented scale ranges.
