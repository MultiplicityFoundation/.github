                                       Abstract
          This paper introduces Trifecta Recursion, a new mathematical frame-
      work that models reality as a recursive triadic fractal. Unlike binary or
      dualistic systems, Trifecta mathematics formalizes the principle that ev-
      ery stable entity emerges from a triadic balance of forces, functions, or
      elements. By treating each trifecta as self-similar and infinitely recursive,
      the model provides a fractal geometry for substance in space—linking
      atomic structures, biological systems, cognition, and architecture under a
      single mathematical law.


2     Introduction
Fractals describe geometric structures that repeat self-similar patterns across
scales (Mandelbrot, 1982). Yet, most existing mathematical systems rely on bi-
nary or dual models that fail to capture triadic balance and recursive emergence.
This paper proposes Trifecta Recursion, a new triadic fractal system grounded
in the principle that stability emerges through sets of three, each of which recur-
sively nests into smaller triads. Applications include physics, material science,
cognition, and architecture.

5     Substance in Space: Fractal Geometry of Mat-
      ter
5.1    Atoms as Trifectas
Proton, neutron, and electron form the atomic trifecta. Each subdivides into
quark-level triads (Griffiths, 2008).

Proteins, fats, and water in food matrices such as cheese demonstrate triadic

5.3    Architecture in Space
Pyramids manifest base, sides, and apex as structural trifectas. Tube-in-tube
skyscrapers mirror recursive trifecta stability (Ali & Moon, 2007).

5.4    Cognition as Trifecta Recursion
Neurodiverse cognition exemplifies heightened recognition of recursive triadic
stacks. Pattern stacking represents fractal recursion in mental space (Murray,
Lesser, & Lawson, 2005; Mottron, 2017).

7     Applications Across Disciplines
    • Physics: Atomic and molecular stability.
    • Engineering: Spiral/tube-in-tube structures, earthquake resilience, aerospace
      stability (Lakes, 1993).

    • Biology: DNA double helix as counter-twisting trifecta recursion (Wat-

    • Cognition: Pattern stacking as mental fractal recursion (Murray et al.,
      2005).

    • Architecture: Pyramids, fractal scaling, and natural flow with stress
      (Bejan, 1997; Ali & Moon, 2007).

Trifecta Recursion provides a new mathematical framework where every struc-
