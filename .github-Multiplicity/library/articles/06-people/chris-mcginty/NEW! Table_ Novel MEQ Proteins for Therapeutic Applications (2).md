1. Insulin Analogs

Objective: Develop insulin analogs with enhanced stability and controlled release for diabetes
management.

Key Strategies:

   ●   Protein Engineering: Modify amino acid sequences to improve stability and reduce
       degradation.
   ●   Formulation Development: Create formulations with long-acting profiles.
   ●   Delivery Systems: Develop controlled release systems for consistent blood sugar
       management.

2. Monoclonal Antibodies for Cancer

Objective: Develop monoclonal antibodies targeting specific cancer markers to improve efficacy
and reduce side effects.

Key Strategies:

   ●   Target Identification: Select specific cancer markers for antibody binding.
   ●   Protein Engineering: Enhance binding affinity and stability.
   ●   Combination Therapy: Combine with other treatments for synergistic effects.

3. Recombinant Enzymes for Genetic Disorders

Objective: Develop recombinant enzymes to replace deficient enzymes in patients with genetic
disorders.

Key Strategies:

   ●   Gene Cloning: Clone genes encoding the deficient enzyme.
   ●   Expression Optimization: Optimize expression systems for high yield and purity.
   ●   Stability Enhancement: Engineer enzymes for enhanced stability in vivo.

4. Cytokines for Immune Modulation

Objective: Develop cytokines that modulate immune responses for treating diseases like
cancer and autoimmune disorders.

Key Strategies:

   ●   Protein Engineering: Enhance stability and reduce immunogenicity.
   ●   Controlled Release: Develop systems for controlled release and targeted delivery.
   ●   Combination Therapy: Use in combination with other immunotherapies.
5. Fusion Proteins for Autoimmune Diseases

Objective: Develop fusion proteins that combine therapeutic domains to treat autoimmune
diseases.

Key Strategies:

   ●   Protein Engineering: Fuse domains with complementary therapeutic effects.
   ●   Stability Testing: Ensure stability and activity of the fusion protein.
   ●   Efficacy Testing: Validate efficacy in preclinical models.

6. Antibody-Drug Conjugates

Objective: Develop antibody-drug conjugates (ADCs) that deliver cytotoxic drugs specifically to
cancer cells.

Key Strategies:

   ●   Antibody Selection: Choose antibodies with high specificity for cancer markers.
   ●   Linker Optimization: Develop linkers that release the drug in the target environment.
   ●   Toxicity Testing: Ensure minimal off-target toxicity.

7. Growth Factors for Regenerative Medicine

Objective: Develop growth factors that promote tissue regeneration and healing.

Key Strategies:

   ●   Protein Engineering: Enhance stability and activity of growth factors.
   ●   Delivery Systems: Develop sustained release systems for continuous delivery.
   ●   Combination Therapy: Use with scaffolds and other biomaterials.

8. Therapeutic Vaccines

Objective: Develop protein-based vaccines that elicit strong immune responses against
infectious diseases and cancer.

Key Strategies:

   ●   Antigen Selection: Choose antigens that elicit strong and specific immune responses.
   ●   Adjuvant Development: Develop adjuvants that enhance vaccine efficacy.
   ●   Delivery Systems: Optimize delivery systems for targeted immune activation.

9. Protein-Based Antivirals

Objective: Develop proteins that inhibit viral replication and spread.
