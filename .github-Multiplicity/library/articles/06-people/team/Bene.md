Plasmoid (in Millerian Terms):

A condensed psychic structure formed by unprocessed trauma, suppressed
emotion, and survival-adapted identity.

In Miller's lens, a plasmoid is the hardened energetic formation of a
child's pain, compressed into socially acceptable roles: the gifted
child, the helper, the perfect one. It is not a self---it is an adaptive
artifact built in environments where the true self was unsafe. Like a
psychic cyst, it stores repressed rage, grief, and unmet needs. The
plasmoid helps you survive. But over time, it distorts you from within.

Miller never used this word literally---but her descriptions of \"false
self\" and \"poisonous pedagogy\" match this exactly. A plasmoid is the
psychic weather pattern created when the child cannot express truth and
must store it inside, frozen in time.

✸ Signal (in Millerian Terms):

The authentic emotional truth of the child---the raw, often unspoken
knowing of what happened, how it felt, and what it cost.

The signal is what the body remembers when the mind forgets. It is
undeniable, primal clarity---the flash of shame, the tightening throat,
the frozen silence in a family dinner. It is not interpreted---it is.

For Miller, the signal is what the child was forced to disown in order
to remain loved---or at least, not abandoned. Her work says: the path to
healing is not interpretation, but feeling the original signal fully, in
the presence of an enlightened witness.

✸ Echo (in Millerian Terms):

The delayed reverberation of the suppressed signal---surfacing as
symptoms, projections, repetitive behavior, or misdirected rage.

The echo is the adult's depression, addiction, codependency, or
numbness. It's the unconscious repetition of trauma---not because they
want to, but because the original signal never reached safe ground.

For Miller, our adult dysfunctions are echoes of truths we weren't
allowed to feel when they first happened. They replay until they are
witnessed. Until the signal is received.

In Short:

Plasmoid: The armored false self that formed around pain.

Signal: The original truth the child could not express.

Echo: The symptom or behavior that cries out in its place.
