### **Quantum GABA Quantum Neuroprocessor**

#### **Overview**

### **Quantum GABA (Gamma-Aminobutyric Acid)** forms the foundation of a **quantum neuroprocessor** designed to model and regulate the brain\'s inhibitory functions. GABA is the primary inhibitory neurotransmitter responsible for reducing neuronal excitability, preventing overstimulation, and maintaining emotional balance. This quantum neuroprocessor leverages **quantum superposition**, **entanglement**, and **quantum gates** to simulate how GABA moderates excitatory signals, interacting with neurotransmitters such as glutamate. This model provides insights into **neural inhibition**, **anxiety regulation**, and **balance between excitation and inhibition**.
