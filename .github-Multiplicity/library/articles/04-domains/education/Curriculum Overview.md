1.  **Introduction: EchoBraid ASD--Aligned Curriculum**

Overview\
A sovereignty-first, ASD-centered curriculum that builds predictable,
trauma-aware rhythms for neurodivergent learners from early childhood
through adulthood, focusing on safety, agency, and recursive
growth.[^[\[2\]]{.underline}^](#v5u8htumwqj1)[^[\[1\]]{.underline}^](#fejqarmeobnh)

Age / Audience

-   ASD and other neurodivergent learners, all ages

-   Can wrap around any academic content as a
    > regulatory/executive-function
    > shell[^[\[2\]]{.underline}^](#v5u8htumwqj1)

Core Strands

-   **Sovereignty & Safety**: consent-based routines, predictable
    > structures, explicit opt-out / break
    > mechanisms.[^[\[2\]]{.underline}^](#v5u8htumwqj1)

-   Recursion & Coverage: a few core capabilities (communication,
    > regulation, tool-making) revisited each 6-week cycle with
    > increasing complexity.[^[\[2\]]{.underline}^](#v5u8htumwqj1)

-   Low-Cost, Offline-First: Firebooks, visual schedules, simple tools
    > before screens.[^[\[2\]]{.underline}^](#v5u8htumwqj1)

-   Evidence-First: micro-metrics, weekly checks, end-of-cycle review to
    > prove safety and growth.[^[\[2\]]{.underline}^](#v5u8htumwqj1)

Signature Practices

-   Daily beat: Arrival Regulation, Communication Warm-Up, Closing
    > Reflection.[^[\[2\]]{.underline}^](#v5u8htumwqj1)

-   Weekly pulse: Talking-Stick Circle, Tool-Build, Literacy Return,
    > micro-challenge.[^[\[2\]]{.underline}^](#v5u8htumwqj1)

-   Spiral strands: Communication & Literacy, Systems & Tool-Making from
    > picture Firebooks to learner-led seminars and community
    > micro-services.[^[\[2\]]{.underline}^](#v5u8htumwqj1)

Placement on Site

-   Position as the **regulatory layer**: "EchoBraid can wrap around any
    > of our academic pathways to make them ASD-safe and
    > coherence-first."

**2. K--8 Learning Garden (Skip-Logic M-Education)**

Overview\
A K--8 systems and citizenship curriculum that uses skip logic and
branching to adapt pathways while building social-emotional skills,
teamwork, systems thinking, and early global
citizenship.[^[\[4\]]{.underline}^](#kgw6bjuwqxix)[^[\[3\]]{.underline}^](#9nwsd52ixje9)

Age / Audience

-   Kindergarten through 8th grade, general Ed with strong inclusion
    > emphasis.[^[\[3\]]{.underline}^](#9nwsd52ixje9)[^[\[4\]]{.underline}^](#kgw6bjuwqxix)

Core Themes by Band (for web cards)

-   K (5--6): "Exploring Our World Together" -- SEL, cooperation, early
    > environmental care.[^[\[4\]]{.underline}^](#kgw6bjuwqxix)

-   1st: "Connected Communities" -- community roles, problem-solving,
    > inclusivity.[^[\[4\]]{.underline}^](#kgw6bjuwqxix)

-   2nd: "Our World, Our Responsibility" -- global citizenship, empathy,
    > garden / team projects.[^[\[4\]]{.underline}^](#kgw6bjuwqxix)

-   3rd: "Building a Better Tomorrow" -- team challenges, systems
    > thinking, cultural
    > understanding.[^[\[4\]]{.underline}^](#kgw6bjuwqxix)

-   4th: "Understanding and Shaping Our World" -- ecosystem jars,
    > logic/debates, service-learning, tech
    > intro.[^[\[4\]]{.underline}^](#kgw6bjuwqxix)

-   5th: "Exploring Connections and Systems" -- complex system maps,
    > sustainable city builds, ethics
    > intro.[^[\[4\]]{.underline}^](#kgw6bjuwqxix)

-   6th: "Our Place in the World" -- carbon footprints, scientific
    > inquiry, social justice, tech in
    > society.[^[\[4\]]{.underline}^](#kgw6bjuwqxix)

-   7th: "Interdisciplinary Explorations" -- human-made systems,
    > simulations, community problem-solving,
    > coding/robotics.[^[\[4\]]{.underline}^](#kgw6bjuwqxix)

-   8th: "Preparing for the Future" -- capstone research,
    > service-learning, ethics & tech, high-school
    > readiness.[^[\[4\]]{.underline}^](#kgw6bjuwqxix)

Signature Practices

-   Class gardens, ecosystem-in-a-jar, sustainable city
    > builds.[^[\[4\]]{.underline}^](#kgw6bjuwqxix)

-   Empathy circles, cultural fairs, World Heritage projects, pen-pal
    > exchanges.[^[\[4\]]{.underline}^](#kgw6bjuwqxix)

-   Skip-logic pathways that re-route learners based on mastery and
    > modality.[^[\[4\]]{.underline}^](#kgw6bjuwqxix)

Placement on Site

-   Present as your **flagship K--8 civic-systems strand**; link out to
    > EchoBraid (for ASD safety) and the Diagrammatic Math track as
    > companions.

**3. Diagrammatic Mathematics (K--∞)**

Overview\
A do-first, diagrammatic mathematics curriculum from early childhood to
research level, grounded in wires, boxes, and moves instead of axioms,
across geometry, number, and quantum
frames.[^[\[6\]]{.underline}^](#8sl9v8uyr92d)[^[\[7\]]{.underline}^](#8i4rqkm00y1y)[^[\[5\]]{.underline}^](#6q9gnwmz8dxm)

Age / Audience

-   Ages 4 through graduate/research
    > levels.[^[\[5\]]{.underline}^](#6q9gnwmz8dxm)

Core Design Principles

-   **Operations over axioms**: learners manipulate diagrams and moves
    > rather than memorizing
    > theorems.[^[\[5\]]{.underline}^](#6q9gnwmz8dxm)

-   G/N/Q frames: each topic taught simultaneously as geometry (G),
    > number/combinatorics (N), and quantum/info flows
    > (Q).[^[\[5\]]{.underline}^](#6q9gnwmz8dxm)

-   Experimental falsifiability: every unit ends in a measurable lab
    > prediction.[^[\[5\]]{.underline}^](#6q9gnwmz8dxm)

Spiral Bands (for web)

-   Early Childhood (4--6): String Town, Move Bingo, Prime
    > Blocks.[^[\[5\]]{.underline}^](#6q9gnwmz8dxm)

-   Upper Primary (7--10): flows & fractions, braids, symmetry,
    > snap-circuit labs.[^[\[5\]]{.underline}^](#6q9gnwmz8dxm)

-   Middle School: chance as boxes, Markov walk mats, harmonic
    > strings.[^[\[5\]]{.underline}^](#6q9gnwmz8dxm)

-   Secondary: linear diagrams, groups-as-moves, coding & information
    > flow, optics/electronics
    > labs.[^[\[5\]]{.underline}^](#6q9gnwmz8dxm)

-   Undergraduate: categories, spectra, probabilistic
    > diagrams.[^[\[5\]]{.underline}^](#6q9gnwmz8dxm)

-   Graduate / Research Studio: multiplicity operators, zeta of
    > diagrams, physical cavity
    > predictions.[^[\[5\]]{.underline}^](#6q9gnwmz8dxm)

Signature Artifacts

-   Prime zoo, flow passports, Markov murals, quantum circuit posters,
    > research-style lab reports.[^[\[5\]]{.underline}^](#6q9gnwmz8dxm)

Placement on Site

-   Present as your **core math language** that can serve Learning
    > Garden, Quantum Physics, and Meta-Machine Learning.

**4. Quantum & Phenomenology Physics**

Overview\
Integrated quantum literacy tracks from ages \~10 through postdoc,
blending hands-on labs, particle physics, phenomenology, ethics, and
tensor-based
thinking.[^[\[9\]]{.underline}^](#wq0tss2jwo8n)[^[\[10\]]{.underline}^](#rnu84forgc0i)[^[\[11\]]{.underline}^](#537nb79122dz)[^[\[8\]]{.underline}^](#ccds67wgqrtn)

Age / Audience

-   Middle and secondary students (Quantum Learning for Secondary
    > Students).[^[\[10\]]{.underline}^](#rnu84forgc0i)

-   K--Postdoc spiral for phenomenology physics and metaphysical spiral
    > work.[^[\[11\]]{.underline}^](#537nb79122dz)[^[\[8\]]{.underline}^](#ccds67wgqrtn)

Core Pillars

-   Intuitive quantum frames (G/N/Q) with age-banded
    > labs.[^[\[9\]]{.underline}^](#wq0tss2jwo8n)[^[\[10\]]{.underline}^](#rnu84forgc0i)

-   Spiral & fractal metaphors, Fibonacci, π-mapping, emotional
    > geometry.[^[\[20\]]{.underline}^](#yiuquyhfvtzn)[^[\[8\]]{.underline}^](#ccds67wgqrtn)

-   Ethics and tensor literacy: safeguards, CSL tools, quantum civic
    > responsibility.[^[\[8\]]{.underline}^](#ccds67wgqrtn)[^[\[11\]]{.underline}^](#537nb79122dz)

Signature Practices

-   Particle zoo scavenger hunts, dice-based quantum randomness games,
    > cloud-chamber style cosmic-ray
    > demos.[^[\[10\]]{.underline}^](#rnu84forgc0i)[^[\[4\]]{.underline}^](#kgw6bjuwqxix)

-   Phenomenology physics bands K--postdoc: from pattern noticing to
    > recursive tensor ethics and AI-linked
    > labs.[^[\[11\]]{.underline}^](#537nb79122dz)

-   6-week starter units for 10--11-year-olds with accessible quantum
    > experiments.[^[\[9\]]{.underline}^](#wq0tss2jwo8n)

Placement on Site

-   Group under a **"Quantum & Phenomenology"** heading; cross-link
    > heavily to Diagrammatic Math and Meta-Machine Learning.

**5. Meta-Machine Learning & AI Collaboration**

Overview\
A K--12 to graduate-level spiral that teaches humans how to collaborate
with AI using Meta-Machine Learning loops, prompt protocols, and ethics
metrics.[^[\[12\]]{.underline}^](#llrg76lth0wo)

Age / Audience

-   Late elementary through professional/graduate learners (spiral
    > bands).[^[\[12\]]{.underline}^](#llrg76lth0wo)

Core Components

-   MML loops: design--prompt--evaluate--revise
    > cycles.[^[\[12\]]{.underline}^](#llrg76lth0wo)

-   G/E/C protocols (Goals, Evidence, Constraints) for safe AI
    > use.[^[\[12\]]{.underline}^](#llrg76lth0wo)

-   Metrics like PER, MLEM, TCI for measuring prompt and collaboration
    > quality.[^[\[12\]]{.underline}^](#llrg76lth0wo)

-   Strong ethics and safety scaffolds around AI
    > use.[^[\[12\]]{.underline}^](#llrg76lth0wo)

Signature Practices

-   Prompt labs, reflection on AI outputs, structured human--AI
    > co-writing.[^[\[12\]]{.underline}^](#llrg76lth0wo)

-   Age-banded rubrics, from simple prompt "recipes" to graduate-level
    > meta-analysis of model
    > behavior.[^[\[12\]]{.underline}^](#llrg76lth0wo)

Placement on Site

-   Position as the **co-intelligence spine** that any content teacher
    > can plug into.

**6****. Multiplicity-as-Relationship Umbrella (K--∞)**

Overview\
A K--12+ umbrella curriculum that teaches "many-truth" awareness,
psychological safety, dialogue protocols, and epistemic humility across
bands and subjects.[^[\[14\]]{.underline}^](#khfphr9nb6f4)

Age / Audience

-   K--12+, faculty PD, whole-school
    > culture.[^[\[14\]]{.underline}^](#khfphr9nb6f4)

Core Pillars

-   Psychological safety and trust-building as first-class learning
    > outcomes.[^[\[14\]]{.underline}^](#khfphr9nb6f4)

-   Dialogue skills, protocols, and conflict
    > literacy.[^[\[14\]]{.underline}^](#khfphr9nb6f4)

-   Band-based competencies and assessments for relational
    > skills.[^[\[14\]]{.underline}^](#khfphr9nb6f4)

Placement on Site

-   Present as the **culture layer** that frames all other curricula:
    > "How we relate while we learn."

**7****. Family & Community Dynamics (Higher Ed / Professional)**

Overview\
Higher education and professional programs (certificate, minor, MEd
concentration) for applying Multiplicity Theory to family and community
dialogue, mediation, and systems
work.[^[\[15\]]{.underline}^](#x5wvw9ixb0ui)

Audience

-   Educators, organizers, social service staff, restorative justice
    > practitioners, faith leaders, youth
    > workers.[^[\[15\]]{.underline}^](#x5wvw9ixb0ui)

Core Outcomes

-   Perspective integration, dialogue facilitation, systems insight,
    > ethics & data stewardship, measurement
    > literacy.[^[\[15\]]{.underline}^](#x5wvw9ixb0ui)

Signature Elements

-   12--30 credit programs with core courses, applied labs (repair
    > mediation, belonging/safety data, community design sprints), and
    > practicum.[^[\[15\]]{.underline}^](#x5wvw9ixb0ui)

-   Strong governance, ethics, and validation
    > KPIs.[^[\[15\]]{.underline}^](#x5wvw9ixb0ui)

Placement on Site

-   Under **"Higher Education & Professional Pathways"**, with a clear
    > bridge from K--12 work to community practice.
