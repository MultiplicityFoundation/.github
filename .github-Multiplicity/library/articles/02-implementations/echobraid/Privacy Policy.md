**Privacy Policy**

*Last updated: \[2/12/2026\]*

ΞchoBraid is designed to honor your signal, not harvest your data. This
Privacy Policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and how you
stay in
control.[^[\[3\]]{.underline}^](#iu91euokzk1x)[^[\[1\]]{.underline}^](#6t189hhukb6n)

**1. Who we are**

ΞchoBraid is a neurodivergent‑affirming learning space for ASD and other
neurodivergent learners, their families, and
educators.[^[\[1\]]{.underline}^](#6t189hhukb6n)[^[\[3\]]{.underline}^](#iu91euokzk1x)\
Our products and curriculum follow the principles of sovereignty,
safety, low‑stimulus design, and user‑controlled
memory.[^[\[2\]]{.underline}^](#otg2yzlbp2gy)[^[\[3\]]{.underline}^](#iu91euokzk1x)

If you have questions about this policy, you can contact us at:

-   Email: \[info\@ΞchoBraid.com\]

-   Phone: \[704-339-8788\]

**2. Core privacy commitments**

We follow a "small data, by consent"
approach:[^[\[2\]]{.underline}^](#otg2yzlbp2gy)[^[\[1\]]{.underline}^](#6t189hhukb6n)

-   By default, we do not save your demo sessions or practice activities
    > unless you explicitly choose to save
    > them.[^[\[1\]]{.underline}^](#6t189hhukb6n)

-   When we do store data, we aim to store the minimum needed, for the
    > shortest reasonable time, as close to you as possible
    > (local‑first).[^[\[2\]]{.underline}^](#otg2yzlbp2gy)[^[\[1\]]{.underline}^](#6t189hhukb6n)

-   You can request deletion of your data, and in many cases can erase
    > it yourself inside the
    > app.[^[\[1\]]{.underline}^](#6t189hhukb6n)[^[\[2\]]{.underline}^](#otg2yzlbp2gy)

**3. What we collect**

**3.1 Information you provide directly**

Depending on how you use ΞchoBraid, you may
share:[^[\[1\]]{.underline}^](#6t189hhukb6n)

-   Account details (for registered users):

    -   Adult's name, email address, and password or sign‑in token

    -   Optional profile info such as role (parent, educator, therapist)

-   Learner information (when an adult sets up a profile):

    -   First name or nickname

    -   Age band or grade level

    -   Optional notes such as preferred communication modes or sensory
        > supports

-   Learning content:

    -   Journal entries, drawings, or uploads

    -   Responses to prompts in Soft Loop, Holo‑Scriptor, or other
        > activities

    -   Lesson plans, checklists, and tools created by educators or
        > learners

-   Communication:

    -   Messages or emails you send us for support

    -   Feedback forms, survey responses, or bug reports

**3.2 Information collected automatically**

To keep the service running and improve it in a privacy‑first way, we
may collect limited technical data, such
as:[^[\[1\]]{.underline}^](#6t189hhukb6n)

-   Device and browser type

-   Approximate region (based on IP, anonymized in analytics)

-   Basic usage events (for example: "Demo viewed", "Curriculum card
    > opened", "PDF downloaded")

We do not use cross‑site tracking, behavioral ad trackers, or
third‑party profiling cookies.[^[\[1\]]{.underline}^](#6t189hhukb6n)

**3.3 Optional sensor and biometric data**

Some experimental features may offer the option to use sensors such as
microphone or heart‑rate variability (HRV) to support regulation or
research. These are always opt‑in and clearly
labeled.[^[\[2\]]{.underline}^](#otg2yzlbp2gy)[^[\[1\]]{.underline}^](#6t189hhukb6n)

-   Microphone input may be used for speech‑to‑text or voice journaling.

-   HRV or similar signals may be used to help reflect on stress and
    > regulation, where supported.

You can see when a sensor is active and can turn it off at any
time.[^[\[2\]]{.underline}^](#otg2yzlbp2gy)[^[\[1\]]{.underline}^](#6t189hhukb6n)

**4. How we use your information**

We use your data to:

-   Provide and support the core app and curriculum features you choose
    > to
    > use.[^[\[3\]]{.underline}^](#iu91euokzk1x)[^[\[1\]]{.underline}^](#6t189hhukb6n)

-   Maintain safety and stability, including detecting abuse or misuse
    > of the
    > platform.[^[\[4\]]{.underline}^](#s16lyqayziqu)[^[\[2\]]{.underline}^](#otg2yzlbp2gy)

-   Improve the quality and accessibility of our content, interfaces,
    > and supports in aggregate
    > form.[^[\[3\]]{.underline}^](#iu91euokzk1x)[^[\[1\]]{.underline}^](#6t189hhukb6n)

-   Communicate with you about updates, changes to this policy, and
    > service information.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use learner data for
targeted
advertising.[^[\[2\]]{.underline}^](#otg2yzlbp2gy)[^[\[1\]]{.underline}^](#6t189hhukb6n)

**5. Data storage modes and retention**

ΞchoBraid offers clear storage choices, presented in‑app where
relevant:[^[\[1\]]{.underline}^](#6t189hhukb6n)

-   **Demo mode (default):**

    -   "Do not save anything" is turned on by default.

    -   When this is active, activities are processed in real time and
        > then discarded.[^[\[1\]]{.underline}^](#6t189hhukb6n)

-   **Erase after 24 hours:**

    -   Content is stored for up to 24 hours, then automatically
        > deleted.[^[\[1\]]{.underline}^](#6t189hhukb6n)

-   **Local‑only:**

    -   Certain data can be stored on a device or local school server
        > under the control of the family or
        > institution.[^[\[2\]]{.underline}^](#otg2yzlbp2gy)[^[\[1\]]{.underline}^](#6t189hhukb6n)

-   **Export:**

    -   You can export some content, such as journals or tools, to PDF
        > or similar formats for your own
        > records.[^[\[1\]]{.underline}^](#6t189hhukb6n)

Where legal or contractual requirements apply (for example, school
agreements or accounting records), we may be required to retain certain
data for longer periods.

**6. Children and minors**

ΞchoBraid is often used with and for children and young people. We
design for their safety from the ground
up:[^[\[3\]]{.underline}^](#iu91euokzk1x)[^[\[1\]]{.underline}^](#6t189hhukb6n)

-   We do not allow children under 13 to create personal accounts
    > without verified adult consent, consistent with applicable child
    > privacy laws.[^[\[1\]]{.underline}^](#6t189hhukb6n)

-   For school deployments, we operate under data‑processing agreements
    > with the institution and follow their consent and record‑keeping
    > requirements.[^[\[4\]]{.underline}^](#s16lyqayziqu)[^[\[1\]]{.underline}^](#6t189hhukb6n)

-   We encourage adults to use pseudonyms, initials, or nicknames for
    > learners whenever full names are not
    > needed.[^[\[5\]]{.underline}^](#af718auwoz1z)[^[\[3\]]{.underline}^](#iu91euokzk1x)

If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child in
violation of applicable law, we will delete it as soon as reasonably
possible.

**7. Sharing and disclosure**

We share personal data only when necessary and with safeguards in place:

-   **Service providers:**\
    > We may use third‑party providers (such as hosting, analytics, or
    > email services) who process data on our behalf under strict
    > contracts that forbid them from using your data for their own
    > purposes.[^[\[1\]]{.underline}^](#6t189hhukb6n)

-   **Schools and organizations:**\
    > For institutional deployments, learner data may be shared with
    > authorized school staff under written agreements and access
    > controls.[^[\[4\]]{.underline}^](#s16lyqayziqu)[^[\[1\]]{.underline}^](#6t189hhukb6n)

-   **Legal requirements and safety:**\
    > We may disclose information if required by law or in response to
    > legal process, or when we believe in good faith that disclosure is
    > necessary to protect someone's safety or prevent serious
    > harm.[^[\[4\]]{.underline}^](#s16lyqayziqu)

We do not sell or rent personal data to third
parties.[^[\[1\]]{.underline}^](#6t189hhukb6n)

**8. Your choices and rights**

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the right
to:

-   Access the personal data we hold about you.

-   Request correction of inaccurate information.

-   Request deletion of your data, subject to legal retention limits.

-   Object to or restrict certain types of processing.

-   Withdraw consent for optional features at any
    > time.[^[\[2\]]{.underline}^](#otg2yzlbp2gy)[^[\[1\]]{.underline}^](#6t189hhukb6n)

You can often exercise these rights directly in the app (for example,
deleting content or choosing a more private storage mode) and can also
contact us for additional help.[^[\[1\]]{.underline}^](#6t189hhukb6n)

**9. Data security**

We use technical and organizational measures designed to protect your
information from unauthorized access, loss, or
misuse.[^[\[4\]]{.underline}^](#s16lyqayziqu)[^[\[2\]]{.underline}^](#otg2yzlbp2gy)\
These measures include encryption in transit, access controls, staff
training, and regular review of our safety
architecture.[^[\[2\]]{.underline}^](#otg2yzlbp2gy)

No system is perfectly secure; if we discover a data incident that
affects your information, we will investigate and notify you and/or
relevant authorities where required by law.

**10. International data transfers**

Depending on your location and where our servers and service providers
are based, your information may be processed in other countries with
different data‑protection laws.[^[\[1\]]{.underline}^](#6t189hhukb6n)\
Where required, we use safeguards such as standard contractual clauses
or equivalent mechanisms to protect personal data transferred across
borders.

**11. Research, metrics, and "small data"**

ΞchoBraid values evidence‑based practice and "small data"
approaches:[^[\[5\]]{.underline}^](#af718auwoz1z)[^[\[3\]]{.underline}^](#iu91euokzk1x)

-   We primarily use aggregate, anonymized analytics to understand usage
    > patterns and improve the
    > platform.[^[\[1\]]{.underline}^](#6t189hhukb6n)

-   If we wish to use identifiable data for research, we will seek
    > explicit consent and, where appropriate, ethics review or
    > institutional
    > approval.[^[\[4\]]{.underline}^](#s16lyqayziqu)[^[\[2\]]{.underline}^](#otg2yzlbp2gy)

-   Any dashboards or experimental "reciprocity" or "coherence" metrics
    > are opt‑in, clearly labeled, and treated as UNPROVEN until
    > validated.[^[\[5\]]{.underline}^](#af718auwoz1z)[^[\[2\]]{.underline}^](#otg2yzlbp2gy)

**12. Changes to this policy**

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes
in our services, technology, or legal
requirements.[^[\[1\]]{.underline}^](#6t189hhukb6n)\
When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date
and, where appropriate, provide additional notice (for example, via
email or in‑app banners).

Continued use of ΞchoBraid after changes take effect means you accept
the updated Policy. If you do not agree with the changes, you should
stop using the service and request deletion of your data where
applicable.

This draft is intentionally plain‑language and sovereignty‑forward; your
legal counsel can tune jurisdiction‑specific clauses (for example,
GDPR/CCPA rights wording) while keeping the core commitments
intact.[^[\[4\]]{.underline}^](#s16lyqayziqu)[^[\[2\]]{.underline}^](#otg2yzlbp2gy)[^[\[1\]]{.underline}^](#6t189hhukb6n)
