**1. Home Routines Starter Kit**
--------------------------------

-   Printable visual schedules, Arrival/Regulation/Warm‑Down scripts,
    > and simple "Echo Routines" you can run in 5--10 minutes each day.

-   Step‑by‑step guidance for introducing choice boards, calm corners,
    > and communication warm‑ups without turning the home into a clinic
    > or a classroom.

**2. Homeschool / Micro‑school Implementation Support**
-------------------------------------------------------

-   Consultation packages to map EchoBraid strands into a family's or
    > pod's existing curriculum (for example, weaving Talking Stick and
    > ToolBuild into language arts, science, or garden projects).

-   Simple data tools families can actually maintain: one‑page weekly
    > check‑ins for transitions, initiations, reading, or tool use,
    > stored locally in binders or offline folders.

**3. Low‑Stimulus App Mode for Home**
-------------------------------------

-   A "home view" of the EchoBraid app with fewer options on screen,
    > family‑friendly language, and clear "do not save" defaults unless
    > a parent opts in.

-   Optional access to the AI Copilot in reflection‑only mode (no
    > advice, just prompts and gentle paraphrasing), with parent‑level
    > control over what's stored or erased.

**4. Resource Library and Stories from Families**
-------------------------------------------------

-   A curated digital library of one‑page stories, checklists, and
    > example routines from other families using EchoBraid, emphasizing
    > many different neurodivergent profiles and home contexts.

-   Templates for families to write and share their own "day in our
    > EchoBraid home" zines, reinforcing that adaptation---not
    > perfection---is the goal.

**Future "Pro Site" services and customized software**
------------------------------------------------------

For schools, districts, and clinics that need deeper infrastructure,
EchoBraid will offer Pro Site tiers and custom builds that extend the
free core app without compromising sovereignty or sensory safety.

**1. Multi‑site administration and governance**
-----------------------------------------------

-   Organization‑level dashboards to manage multiple campuses, pods, or
    > programs from a single control panel.​

-   Role‑based access (for example, teacher, therapist, coordinator,
    > admin) with clear permissions around who can view, export, or
    > delete which records.​

-   Site‑specific settings for cadence, strands in use, and which
    > UNPROVEN features (if any) are enabled, so each site can match its
    > own risk and ethics policies.

**2. Local node hosting and data sovereignty**
----------------------------------------------

-   On‑premise or region‑local "EchoBraid Node" hosting so portfolios,
    > metrics, and logs live under the institution's control, not in a
    > central cloud.

-   Configurable retention rules (for example, erase after 24 hours,
    > term‑bound storage, or long‑term archives) with institution‑level
    > and learner‑level deletion options.​

-   Optional integration with EchoThread‑style safety modules, still
    > running locally, to support AI‑assisted reflection while
    > maintaining user‑controlled memory.

**3. Advanced but "small" reporting**
-------------------------------------

-   Simple, human‑readable dashboards showing trends in regulation
    > success, communication attempts, tool use, and
    > participation---never scores or behaviorist labels.​

-   Cohort‑level views to support program improvement (for example,
    > changes in transition success or question‑to‑answer ratios) while
    > keeping individual data minimal and de‑identified where possible.​

-   Export to PDF or CSV for IEP meetings, family conferences, or
    > internal reviews, with explicit consent flows before sharing
    > beyond the local node.

**4. Custom curriculum bundles and integrations**
-------------------------------------------------

-   Tailored EchoBraid bundles for specific contexts: early‑grades
    > autism classrooms, trauma‑aware alternative programs,
    > justice‑linked or Inside‑Out learning, community gardens, or
    > higher‑ed labs.

-   White‑label or co‑branded interfaces that preserve EchoBraid's
    > low‑stimulus, consent‑first patterns while matching a partner's
    > visual identity.​

-   API hooks or flat‑file sync so EchoBraid routines and metrics can be
    > referenced inside existing SIS/LMS systems without copying full
    > learner histories into third‑party clouds.
