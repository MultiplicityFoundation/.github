Time is the fourth dimension, and there may exist a space timeline with
variations in the 4D world. Humans typically choose variations within
the range of 0.1 to nearly 1. Choosing a variation beyond 1 or below 0.1
is nearly impossible.

Since these variations can lead to different timeline paths, they
influence the future in different ways. As they are infinite in number,
the possible outcomes are also infinite.
