**Abstract:**

Multiplicity within the realm of Social Physics, introduces a novel
perspective that redefines the understanding of social interactions. At
its core, the theory posits that the dynamics of human engagement are
multifaceted and can be encapsulated through the concept of
\"Multiplicity.\" Unlike traditional models that often oversimplify
complex human behaviors, Multiplicity Theory acknowledges and embraces
the diverse, intricate nature of social connections.
