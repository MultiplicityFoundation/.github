 vacuum energy extraction, leveraging prime-indexed resonance effects to enhance energy efficiency
 and field interactions. The system is designed to remain within fundamental physical constraints,
 ensuring energy conservation and adherence to quantum field dynamics.

 By bridging quantum gravity, AI-optimized propulsion, and prime-encoded tensor fields, PBQG
 establishes a scalable framework for next-generation space travel. Future work includes
 computational validation, AI-driven tensor evolution studies, and prototype testing of
 vacuum-assisted propulsion systems.
