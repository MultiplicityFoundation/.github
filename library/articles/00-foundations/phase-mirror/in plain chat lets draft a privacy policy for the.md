**Privacy Policy for Phase Mirror**

**Effective Date:** \[Date\]\
**Last Updated:** \[Date\]

**1. Introduction**

This Privacy Policy describes how Proof Labs LLC (\"Phase Mirror,\"
\"we,\" \"us,\" or \"our\") collects, uses, discloses, and protects your
personal information when you use our website, SaaS platform (the
\"Phase Mirror Oracle\"), consulting services, training programs, and
certification
programs.[^[\[1\]]{.underline}^](#fn1)[^[\[2\]]{.underline}^](#fn2)

**2. Information We Collect**

**Information You Provide Directly:**

-   Account registration data (name, email, company, job title)

-   Payment and billing information

-   Communications you send us (support requests, feedback)

-   Certification and training enrollment
    information[^[\[2\]]{.underline}^](#fn2)

**Information Collected Automatically:**

-   Usage data (features accessed, timestamps, rule executions)

-   Device and browser information

-   IP addresses and general location data

-   Cookies and similar tracking technologies

**Platform-Specific Data:**

-   Dissonance reports and governance artifacts generated through the
    Oracle

-   Configuration files and rule customizations

-   Calibration data (aggregated false-positive rates used to improve
    rule accuracy---collected only with
    consent)[^[\[3\]]{.underline}^](#fn3)

**3. How We Use Your Information**

We use collected information to:

-   Provide and maintain the Phase Mirror platform and services

-   Process transactions and manage subscriptions across Community,
    Team, Business, and Enterprise tiers[^[\[2\]]{.underline}^](#fn2)

-   Deliver consulting, implementation, and training
    services[^[\[1\]]{.underline}^](#fn1)

-   Improve platform accuracy through aggregated calibration data
    (opt-in only)[^[\[3\]]{.underline}^](#fn3)

-   Administer certification programs and verify credentials

-   Send service-related communications and, with consent, marketing
    materials

-   Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms

**4. Calibration Data and Network Effects**

The Phase Mirror platform may collect aggregated, anonymized calibration
data to improve rule accuracy across the ecosystem. This
data:[^[\[3\]]{.underline}^](#fn3)

-   Is anonymized before aggregation

-   Cannot be used to identify individual users or organizations

-   May be disabled by Enterprise customers upon request

-   Creates value for all platform users through improved false-positive
    calibration

**5. Information Sharing and Disclosure**

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

-   **Service Providers:** Third parties who assist with hosting,
    payment processing, and support

-   **Certified Partners:** Only with your explicit consent when
    engaging certified consulting partners[^[\[2\]]{.underline}^](#fn2)

-   **Legal Requirements:** When required by law, court order, or to
    protect our rights

-   **Business Transfers:** In connection with a merger, acquisition, or
    sale of assets

-   **Citizen Gardens Foundation:** Governance oversight as part of our
    Mozilla-model structure[^[\[3\]]{.underline}^](#fn3)

**6. Data Retention**

We retain personal information:

-   **Account Data:** For the duration of your account plus \[X\] years

-   **Transaction Records:** As required by applicable tax and financial
    regulations

-   **Certification Records:** Indefinitely to verify credential
    authenticity

-   **Usage/Calibration Data:** Aggregated data retained indefinitely;
    identifiable data deleted within \[X\] months of account closure

**7. Your Rights and Choices**

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

-   Access the personal information we hold about you

-   Correct inaccurate information

-   Request deletion of your data

-   Opt out of calibration data collection

-   Export your data in a portable format

-   Withdraw consent for marketing communications

To exercise these rights, contact us at \[privacy\@phasemirror.io\].

**8. Security**

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

-   Encryption in transit and at rest

-   Access controls and authentication

-   Regular security audits (especially for Enterprise tier with 99.9%
    SLA commitments)[^[\[2\]]{.underline}^](#fn2)

-   Incident response procedures

**9. International Data Transfers**

If you are located outside the United States, your information may be
transferred to and processed in the U.S. We use appropriate safeguards
for international transfers, including Standard Contractual Clauses
where applicable.

**10. Children\'s Privacy**

Phase Mirror services are not directed to individuals under 16. We do
not knowingly collect personal information from children.

**11. Changes to This Policy**

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of
material changes via email or prominent notice on our website.
