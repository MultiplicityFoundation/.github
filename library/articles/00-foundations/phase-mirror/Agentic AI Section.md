### Autonomy vs. Governance Control {#autonomy-vs.-governance-control .tension-title}

True autonomy requires permission to fail and learn, yet enterprise
governance demands predictable, deterministic success with zero-failure
tolerance.

::: {.tension-impact}
**Impact:** Stalled innovation or unacceptable liability exposure

### No-Code Interface vs. Causal Expertise {#no-code-interface-vs.-causal-expertise .tension-title}

to build valid causal models, leading to deployment of faulty logic.

### Governance by Hope {#governance-by-hope .approach-title}

-   Abstract \"alignment\" goals without binding mechanisms
-   \"Collaborate\" without defined forum, cadence, or quorum
-   Vague responsibility assignments with no clear owner
-   Impressionistic \"vibe claims\" instead of measurable outcomes
-   Hope as strategy for managing AI autonomy risks
:::

::: {.approach-card .right}
[Phase Mirror Approach]{.approach-badge}

### Governance by Design {#governance-by-design .approach-title}

-   Binding artifacts: specs, contracts, SLAs, kill-switches
-   Explicit mechanisms with defined structure and triggers
-   Named owners with measurable metrics and time horizons
-   Concrete levers tested incrementally in production
-   Auditable governance trail from policy to practice
:::
