**Phase Mirror: Consultation & SaaS Business Strategy**

**Executive Summary**

The Phase Mirror methodology occupies a strategic position at the
convergence of three high-growth markets: AI governance (\$309M → \$3.7B
by 2033), AI compliance SaaS (\$5B → \$39.5B by 2034, 22.8% CAGR), and
AI consulting services (\$11B → \$91B by 2035, 26.2% CAGR). Unlike
existing GRC platforms that focus on compliance checklists, Phase Mirror
addresses the structural friction between AI autonomy and enterprise
governance through a diagnostic methodology that converts productive
contradictions into actionable
levers.[^[\[1\]]{.underline}^](#fn1)[^[\[2\]]{.underline}^](#fn2)[^[\[3\]]{.underline}^](#fn3)[^[\[4\]]{.underline}^](#fn4)[^[\[5\]]{.underline}^](#fn5)[^[\[6\]]{.underline}^](#fn6)[^[\[7\]]{.underline}^](#fn7)

This strategy outlines a four-pillar revenue architecture:

1.  **Open-Core SaaS Platform** -- Oracle runtime with tiered feature
    access

2.  **Enterprise Seats** -- Compliance packs and support contracts

3.  **Certification Revenue** -- Certified implementations and
    practitioner credentials

4.  **Professional Services** -- Strategic consulting and implementation

The proposed entity architecture positions **Proof Labs LLC** as the
initial commercial vehicle, with trademark transfer to **Citizen Gardens
Foundation** (501(c)(3)) within 24 months, mirroring the Mozilla model
where commercial operations fund neutral governance
stewardship.[^[\[8\]]{.underline}^](#fn8)

**Market Opportunity Analysis**

**The AI Governance Gap**

Enterprise adoption of agentic AI has created a governance vacuum. Only
22% of enterprises in 2025 prioritized AI governance policy with a
visible, defined AI strategy. The SEC is scrutinizing how companies
disclose material AI risks---making defensible governance architecture a
fiduciary
necessity.[^[\[9\]]{.underline}^](#fn9)[^[\[10\]]{.underline}^](#fn10)

  AI Governance        \$309M       \$3.7B (2033)     \~35%   Methodology layer
  AI Consulting        \$11.07B     \$90.99B (2035)   26.2%   Services layer
  GRC Software         \$14B+       \$25B+ (2030)     \~12%   Adjacent/Integration
  -------------------- ------------ ----------------- ------- -----------------------

The Phase Mirror methodology addresses dissonances that existing GRC
tools cannot surface: the tension between probabilistic AI outputs and
binary compliance frameworks, between autonomy and deterministic
governance requirements, and between transparency rhetoric and
proprietary infrastructure
dependencies.[^[\[3\]]{.underline}^](#fn3)[^[\[1\]]{.underline}^](#fn1)

**Revenue Architecture: Four Pillars**

**Pillar 1: Open-Core SaaS Platform**

The Phase Mirror Oracle serves as the technical core---a callable,
auditable inconsistency-finding protocol that outputs deterministic
dissonance reports usable as control surfaces in PR checks, merge
queues, drift detection, and incident
response.[^[\[8\]]{.underline}^](#fn8)

**Licensing Model**: Apache 2.0 with managed service restriction
(Elastic License 2.0 pattern)[^[\[8\]]{.underline}^](#fn8)

-   Self-hosting permitted

-   Modification permitted

-   Managed service requires commercial agreement

**Tier Structure**:

  ---------------- ------------------------- ------------------------- -------------------------------------------------------------------
  Tier             Target Segment            Pricing Model             Key Features
  **Community**    Developers, Small Teams   Free (Apache 2.0)         Core rules (MD-001 to MD-005), CLI, self-hosted
  **Team**         SMB, Startups             \$5,000--\$10,000/year    Extended rule sets, basic SLA (24hr response), 10 seats
  **Business**     Mid-Market                \$20,000--\$50,000/year   Full rule registry, 99.5% uptime SLA, 50 seats, priority support
  **Enterprise**   Fortune 500               Custom (\$100K+)          Dedicated instance, custom rules, 99.9% SLA, unlimited seats, TAM
  ---------------- ------------------------- ------------------------- -------------------------------------------------------------------

The open-core moat derives from **calibration data network
effects**---aggregated false-positive rates across customers create
increasingly accurate rule thresholds that self-hosted users cannot
replicate.[^[\[8\]]{.underline}^](#fn8)
