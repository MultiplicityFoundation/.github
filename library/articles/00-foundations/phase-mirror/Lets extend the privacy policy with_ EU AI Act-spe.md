**Lets extend the privacy policy with: EU AI Act-specific provisions for
customers using that pack​**

HIPAA Business Associate language for Healthcare customers\
Financial services data handling provisions

Here are the extended provisions to add to the Privacy Policy:

**Addendum A: EU AI Act Compliance Provisions**

*Applicable to customers using the EU AI Act Readiness Compliance
Pack*[^[\[1\]]{.underline}^](#fn1)

**A.1 Purpose and Scope**

These provisions apply when you use Phase Mirror services to process
data related to AI systems deployed in or affecting individuals in the
European Union. The EU AI Act Readiness Compliance Pack includes risk
classification engines, conformity assessment templates, and high-risk
AI documentation tools.[^[\[1\]]{.underline}^](#fn1)

**A.2 AI System Transparency**

When Phase Mirror processes data about your AI systems for EU AI Act
compliance purposes, we commit to:

-   **Documentation:** Maintaining records of processing activities
    related to your AI system assessments

-   **Transparency:** Providing clear information about how the Platform
    analyzes and classifies your AI systems

-   **Human Oversight:** Ensuring Dissonance Reports and risk
    classifications support---rather than replace---human
    decision-making in conformity assessments

**A.3 High-Risk AI System Data**

If you use Phase Mirror to document or assess high-risk AI systems (as
defined under EU AI Act Article 6), we will:

-   Process technical documentation data only as necessary to provide
    compliance assessment services

-   Maintain data quality standards appropriate for regulatory
    documentation

-   Support your obligations to maintain logs and records for the
    duration required by the EU AI Act (minimum 10 years for high-risk
    systems)

-   Enable data export in formats suitable for regulatory submission

**A.4 Prohibited AI Practices**

Phase Mirror will not knowingly process data to support AI systems that
fall under prohibited practices (EU AI Act Article 5), including:

-   Social scoring systems

-   Real-time remote biometric identification in public spaces (except
    as permitted)

-   Exploitation of vulnerabilities of specific groups

-   Subliminal manipulation techniques

**A.5 Data Localization**

For customers requiring EU data residency:

-   Enterprise tier customers may request a dedicated EU-hosted
    instance[^[\[1\]]{.underline}^](#fn1)

-   Calibration data from EU customers will not be transferred outside
    the EU without appropriate safeguards

-   Upon request, we will provide documentation of data flows for your
    conformity assessments

**A.6 Conformity Assessment Support**

Phase Mirror maintains records sufficient to support your obligations
under:

-   Article 9 (Risk Management System documentation)

-   Article 11 (Technical Documentation)

-   Article 12 (Record-Keeping)

-   Article 13 (Transparency requirements)

**A.7 Incident Notification**

If we become aware of any security incident affecting data used for your
EU AI Act compliance:

-   We will notify you within 48 hours

-   We will provide information necessary for your regulatory
    notifications

-   We will cooperate with any supervisory authority inquiries

**Addendum B: HIPAA Business Associate Provisions**

*Applicable to Healthcare & Life Sciences Compliance Pack customers who
are Covered Entities or Business Associates under
HIPAA*[^[\[1\]]{.underline}^](#fn1)

**B.1 Business Associate Relationship**

When Phase Mirror receives, creates, maintains, or transmits Protected
Health Information (PHI) on behalf of a Covered Entity or Business
Associate customer, Phase Mirror acts as a Business Associate under 45
CFR § 160.103.

**B.2 Permitted Uses and Disclosures**

Phase Mirror will only use or disclose PHI:

-   As necessary to perform services under our agreement

-   As required by law

-   For the proper management and administration of Phase Mirror,
    provided disclosures are required by law or we obtain reasonable
    assurances of confidentiality

-   To report violations of law to appropriate authorities

-   For data aggregation services relating to your healthcare operations
    (if specified in your agreement)

**B.3 Prohibited Uses**

Phase Mirror will not:

-   Use or disclose PHI for marketing purposes

-   Sell PHI

-   Use or disclose PHI in a manner that would violate HIPAA if done by
    the Covered Entity

-   Use genetic information for underwriting purposes

**B.4 Safeguards**

Phase Mirror will implement and maintain:

  ---------------- ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Safeguard Type   Measures
  Administrative   Workforce training, access management policies, security officer designation, risk assessments
  Physical         Facility access controls, workstation security, device and media controls
  Technical        Access controls, audit controls, integrity controls, transmission security (encryption)
  ---------------- ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

**B.5 Minimum Necessary Standard**

Phase Mirror will limit PHI use, disclosure, and requests to the minimum
necessary to accomplish the intended purpose, except for:

-   Disclosures to or requests by healthcare providers for treatment

-   Disclosures to the individual who is the subject of the PHI

-   Uses or disclosures made pursuant to a valid authorization

-   Disclosures to HHS for compliance investigations

-   Uses or disclosures required by law

**B.6 Individual Rights Support**

Phase Mirror will, within 10 business days of request:

-   Provide access to PHI in a Designated Record Set to enable your
    response to individual access requests

-   Make PHI available for amendment and incorporate amendments as
    directed

-   Provide information needed for accountings of disclosures

-   Support restrictions on use/disclosure if agreed to by you

**B.7 Subcontractors**

Phase Mirror will ensure any subcontractors who create, receive,
maintain, or transmit PHI agree in writing to the same restrictions and
conditions that apply to Phase Mirror.

**B.8 Breach Notification**

In the event of a breach of unsecured PHI:

  ----------------------- -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Requirement             Commitment
  Discovery               Phase Mirror will be deemed to have discovered a breach when known to any employee, officer, or agent
  Notification Timeline   Within 30 days of discovery
  Notification Content    Identification of affected individuals, description of PHI involved, recommended mitigation steps
  Investigation Support   Full cooperation with your breach investigation and risk assessment
  Documentation           Maintenance of breach documentation for 6 years
  ----------------------- -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

**B.9 Termination**

Upon termination of the agreement:

-   Phase Mirror will return or destroy all PHI, if feasible

-   If return or destruction is not feasible, protections will extend to
    retained PHI indefinitely

-   Phase Mirror will certify destruction or explain why destruction is
    not feasible

**B.10 Regulatory Access**

Phase Mirror will make internal practices, books, and records relating
to PHI use and disclosure available to the Secretary of HHS for
compliance determination purposes.

**B.11 HIPAA-Specific Platform Features**

For Healthcare & Life Sciences Compliance Pack customers, the Platform
includes:[^[\[1\]]{.underline}^](#fn1)

-   HIPAA AI provisions rule sets

-   FDA Software as a Medical Device (SaMD) guidance alignment

-   Clinical decision support documentation templates

-   Audit logging compliant with 45 CFR § 164.312(b)

**Addendum C: Financial Services Data Handling Provisions**

*Applicable to Financial Services Compliance Pack
customers*[^[\[1\]]{.underline}^](#fn1)

**C.1 Scope and Applicability**

These provisions apply to customers in the financial services industry
using Phase Mirror for AI governance, including banks, broker-dealers,
investment advisers, insurance companies, and other regulated financial
institutions.

**C.2 SEC AI Disclosure Support**

Phase Mirror processes data to support your compliance with SEC
requirements for material AI risk disclosure. We
will:[^[\[2\]]{.underline}^](#fn2)

-   Maintain records of AI system assessments for the duration required
    by securities regulations

-   Provide audit trails suitable for regulatory examination

-   Support your ability to demonstrate defensible governance
    architecture

**C.3 Model Risk Management (SR 11-7 Alignment)**

For customers subject to Federal Reserve SR 11-7 guidance on model risk
management, Phase Mirror supports:

  ------------------- ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  SR 11-7 Element     Phase Mirror Support
  Model Development   Documentation of AI system design decisions and governance choices
  Model Validation    Dissonance Reports identifying gaps between stated governance and implementation
  Model Use           Tracking of production deployment and monitoring commitments
  Governance          Audit trails of policy decisions and approval workflows
  ------------------- ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------

**C.4 Fair Lending Compliance**

The Financial Services Compliance Pack includes fair lending rules. When
processing data related to lending AI
systems:[^[\[1\]]{.underline}^](#fn1)

-   Phase Mirror will not use Customer Data to make or influence credit
    decisions

-   Dissonance Reports may identify potential fair lending risks but do
    not constitute legal advice

-   Data processed for fair lending analysis will be handled with
    heightened access controls

**C.5 Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act (GLBA) Alignment**

Phase Mirror maintains safeguards consistent with GLBA requirements:

**Safeguards Rule Compliance:**

-   Designation of personnel responsible for information security

-   Risk assessment procedures for Customer Data

-   Design and implementation of safeguards

-   Regular testing and monitoring

-   Oversight of service providers

-   Evaluation and adjustment of security programs

**C.6 Non-Public Personal Information (NPI)**

If Customer Data includes NPI as defined under GLBA:

-   Phase Mirror will not disclose NPI to non-affiliated third parties
    except as permitted by GLBA

-   NPI will not be used for calibration data aggregation unless fully
    de-identified per GLBA standards

-   Access to NPI is limited to personnel with legitimate business need

**C.7 Data Retention for Financial Services**

  -------------------------- ------------------------------------ ------------------------------------
  Data Category              Retention Period                     Basis
  AI System Assessments      7 years minimum                      SEC Books and Records requirements
  Model Validation Records   7 years or model lifetime +2 years   SR 11-7 guidance
  Fair Lending Analysis      5 years minimum                      ECOA/Regulation B
  Audit Logs                 7 years                              General securities compliance
  -------------------------- ------------------------------------ ------------------------------------

**C.8 Examination and Audit Rights**

Phase Mirror will:

-   Cooperate with regulatory examinations (SEC, OCC, Federal Reserve,
    state regulators) as required

-   Provide access to relevant records upon reasonable notice

-   Support your internal audit functions with necessary documentation

-   Maintain SOC 2 Type II certification (Enterprise tier)

**C.9 Third-Party Risk Management**

We understand financial institutions must manage third-party risk. Phase
Mirror provides:

-   Annual SOC 2 Type II reports (available to Business and Enterprise
    customers)

-   Responses to standardized third-party risk questionnaires (SIG,
    CAIQ)

-   Documentation of our information security program upon request

-   30-day notice of material changes to our security practices

**C.10 Business Continuity**

Phase Mirror maintains business continuity and disaster recovery
capabilities:

  ------------ ------------------------- --------------------------
  Tier         Recovery Time Objective   Recovery Point Objective
  Team         24 hours                  24 hours
  Business     8 hours                   4 hours
  Enterprise   4 hours                   1 hour
  ------------ ------------------------- --------------------------

**C.11 Data Residency for Financial Services**

Enterprise tier financial services customers may request:

-   U.S.-only data processing and storage

-   Dedicated infrastructure isolated from other customers

-   Enhanced logging and access monitoring

-   Custom data handling procedures per regulatory requirements

**C.12 Incident Response for Financial Services**

In the event of a security incident affecting financial services
Customer Data:

  -------------------- ----------------- -----------------------------------------------------------------
  Step                 Timeline          Action
  Detection            Immediate         Automated monitoring and alerting
  Assessment           Within 4 hours    Severity classification and scope determination
  Notification         Within 24 hours   Customer notification for material incidents
  Regulatory Support   As needed         Support for your regulatory notifications (varies by regulator)
  Remediation          Ongoing           Root cause analysis and corrective action
  Documentation        Within 30 days    Incident report and lessons learned
  -------------------- ----------------- -----------------------------------------------------------------
