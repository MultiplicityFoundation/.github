### Expanding African Mathematics with Multiplicity Theory

African mathematics, deeply rooted in diverse cultural practices,
emphasizes fractal geometry, recursive systems, proportional reasoning,
and practical problem-solving across a range of domains. Integrating
**African mathematical traditions** with **Multiplicity Theory**---a
framework for interconnectedness, non-linearity, and recursive
dynamics---offers a transformative approach to modern computation,
cryptography, and complex systems modeling.

### Core Concepts of African Mathematics

1.  **Fractal Geometry**:

    -   African systems frequently use fractal designs in architecture,
        > art, and governance. These recursive patterns scale across
        > different dimensions.

2.  **Proportional Reasoning**:

    -   Ratios and proportions are used in land allocation, trade, and
        > community decision-making.

3.  **Iterative Problem-Solving**:

    -   Methods rely on iterative refinement, such as repeated halving
        > for measurements or arithmetic operations.

4.  **Symbolic Representation**:

    -   Numbers and mathematical ideas are often expressed through
        > symbolic forms, patterns, and oral traditions.

5.  **Practical Arithmetic**:

    -   Emphasis on utility for agriculture, astronomy, and trade,
        > including modular systems for cyclical phenomena.
