Introduction

Citizen Gardens is a Not For Profit Mutual Benefit Organization
maintained by its participating members and orchestrated by their
elected officials to delegate and secure the accounts and assets of our
community and it remains to constantly research and develop ways to
systematically utilize our intellectual and natural resources to provide
the greatest services and benefits to our communities.

In these efforts this organization was founded and dedicated to the
health and welfare of its members and their greater communities, as we
raise the standards of the industry, our quality of life, and our level
of stewardship towards our global environment and its resources. The
biggest question faced by members is:

**What resources do I really have the capability to offer?**

The structure and function of this organization is similar to that of a
hot air balloon. So that we may weather the winds of today and prepare
for the various winds we may face tomorrow. As an organization dedicated

future for your community.

Administration

The core of our organization is delegated by a board of directors
through which all of the powers of the corporation are administered by
officials who have been elected to office by the members according to
the rules and guidelines contained herein.

**Departments** are created and developed to utilize and replenish
resources, provide membership services, and carry out the administrative
tasks needed to maintain and secure the benefits of our communities.

11

**Managers:** Qualified members who complete our entry level management
program are given the responsibility to oversee the operations of a
department and the members who perform its essential tasks.

**General Managers:** Are qualified by the board of directors and
elected annually to a term of 3 years upon the majority vote of their
department first year as GM-elect, second year as GM, third year as past
GM and oversee the departments within a city.

**District Managers:** Are qualified by the board of directors and
elected annually to a term of 3 years upon the majority vote of their
department first year as DM-elect, second year as DM, third year as past
DM and oversee the offices within a county.

**Regional Managers:** Are qualified by the board of directors and
elected annually to a term of 3 years upon the majority vote of their
department first year as RM-elect, second year as RM, third year as past
RM and oversee and provide support to the offices within a specific
geographical location.

**Officers & Vice Presidents:** For every type of department there is an
Officer and a Vice President who are qualified by the board of directors
to a term of four years. Officers are elected by the majority vote of
the Associates within their department, while VP's are elected according
to the majority vote of all the members. Officers are responsible for
the compliance of internal protocols and procedures, while VP's are
responsible for the compliance of local laws and regulatory
requirements. The board may qualify and appoint Officers as they see
necessary, however any Officer qualified other than to oversee a
department will not be given a seat on the board of directors, but may
participate in other committees and functions as set forth in this hand
book.

**The Presidential Cabinet** is comprised of agents and officers who
serve to assist the Presidents with the various tasks they are required
to perform. Including, but not limited to a secretary, accounts
receivable and expendable, human resources, and security.

13

**President Elect** is a member qualified by the Board of Directors and
elected by the majority vote of all the members to a three-year term.
(First year as President Elect, second year as President, and third year
as Past President) The President-Elect oversees the daily operations of
the VP offices, insuring the compliance of regulatory requirements and
procedures throughout the offices.

**The President** **is the** presiding chairman of the board, conducting
its business in an orderly and timely fashion. When the board is not in
session, duties include acting as the primary representative and
spokesperson for any and all public relations.

**Past President:** As the presiding officer over the presidential
cabinet their main duty is to insure the compliance of internal
protocols and procedures throughout organizations corporate security
divisions, and programs. Implementing and maintaining security processes
to reduce risks, respond to incidents, and limit exposure to liability
in all areas of financial, physical, and personal risk; establish
appropriate standards and risk controls associated with intellectual
property.

Participation Rewards

**C**itizen **G**ardens **C**redits are only recognized by this
organization and by its members and has no affiliation of any kind to
any other form of currency, monetary instrument, or credit system.
Members can redeem their credits to receive exclusive services provided
to our community in accordance to these rules and guidelines which
govern their membership and are outlined in the articles of
incorporation and these bylaws. Every member is rewarded for their
participation as follows:

Points Of Reward

**Ingenuity** credits are the most esteemed and sought after rewards and
is potentially the most valuable rewards a member can receive and tend
to be a point of reward most commonly in competition over. As a research
and development organization we are constantly looking for ways to make
better use of our resources so that we can maximize our productivity and
the potential of our community. One of the ways we do this is by
rewarding our members for their ingenuity.

A good idea in action can optimize the activities of today,
revolutionize the systems of tomorrow, potentially change the course of
history over time, and has no problem finding support or reward.
Ingenuity rewards incur credit based on the difference between
productivity before and after implementation and or how often it is used
across the network. Ingenuity rewards are distributed on a monthly basis
after a 120 day accumulation period starting the day of implementation.

19

**Opportunity** credits, nothing works if we don't, so our aim is to
continuously have a variety of opportunities blended between time slots
and full time positions, it takes people to get things done and it's to
the honor of our hard working volunteers that we recognize and reward
them for their time and effort. Opportunity rewards are distributed on a
weekly basis and are incurred based on the value of the services
provided by the opportunity.

**Designation** credits are incurred monthly by members who are
rightfully able and willing to designate assets such as land,
facilities, or intellectual property to be under the stewardship of the
organization and used only for purposes approved by the designating
member and the Board of Directors. Designation rewards incur credit
according to the services and productivity that are a direct result of
the designation.

**Referral** credits are one of the most valuable annually rewarded
credits that any and all members can receive by referring 9 new members.
For the first 4 referrals a member will receive an annual 5% credit
based on the total credits incurred by each referred account and a 1%
credit for the next 5 referred accounts, for as long as the memberships
are active. Referral rewards are distributed within 120 days from the
first of the year and are the only credits that are non-transferable,
and non-exchangeable.

**Sponsorship** credits always represent the value of a monetary gift
and to show our appreciation this organization will always value a gift
beyond what others say its' worth. We reward our sponsors according to
the law of multiplicity and use this formula 2S+1 as the basis for
exchange. Sponsorship credits do not incur interest; upon cash out any
outstanding sponsorship credits are reimbursed.

Our goal as an organization is to always keep the greatest value and
benefits within our community while at the same time provide beneficial
services to the public. Because the greatest benefit is being a member
and the greatest rewards are the services provided to its members. For
every dollar you pledge by donation or sponsorship you will receive
twice its value in membership credits.

Rewarding Rules

As a member you receive an account which keeps track of your
participation and allows you to easily manage your membership programs,
opportunities, and benefits. Your account can be credited, debited, and
rewarded for your participation. CGC serves as a means for us to
recognize and reward the efforts and participation of our individual
members and to promote a greater level of involvement and value within
our community.

**Exchangeable Credits:** Members may be provided with the ability to
exchange CGC for local currency under the following rules:

-   Youth members between the ages of 14 -18 may not exchange more than
    \$500 a month, with parental or guardian consent.

-   Members can exchange no more than \$2000 a month.

-   Management can exchange no more than \$3000 a month.

-   Vice-Presidents and Officers can exchange no more than \$4000 a
    month.

-   Presidents can exchange no more than \$5000 a month.

Upon dissolution of membership if the total monetary value of
exchangeable credits is more than \$100,000 it will be distributed in
deferred monthly payments unless otherwise determined by the board of
directors.

**Passable Credits:** Passing credits from one member to another may not
exceed 2000 credits a month per recipient.

**Redeeming Credits:** There is no limit on the amount of credits a
member can redeem for membership services.

**Transferable Credits:** Upon dissolution of a membership in any case
these types of credits can be transferred in whole or in part to another
member, members, or may be dedicated to any charitable purpose within
the organization.

In [mathematics](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematics),
the **multiplicity** of a member of
a [multiset](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multiset) is the number of
times it appears in the multiset. For example, the number of times a
given [polynomial
equation](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polynomial_equation) has
a [root](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Root_of_a_function) at a given
point.

The notion of multiplicity is important to be able to count correctly
without specifying exceptions (for example, *double roots* counted
twice). Hence the expression, \"counted with (sometimes implicit)
multiplicity\".

If multiplicity is ignored, this may be emphasized by counting the
number of **distinct** elements, as in \"the number of distinct roots\".
However, whenever a set (as opposed to multiset) is formed, multiplicity
is automatically ignored, without requiring use of the term
\"distinct\".
