**Members:**  A member is anyone and everyone, as we are all individuals
of the same energy (life force) and members of a greater community.
There are two types of members those who multiply (paired) and those who
do not (unpaired). Whether you are paired or unpaired is not so much a
matter of participation as it is a matter of being paired with a
\"multiset\" community who defines the value of maximum multiplicity by
the prime factors of reciprocity between its members.

**Unpaired:** Every individual is a member (naturally unpaired) and has
something to offer as a member of society which is a \"set\" (as opposed
to multiset) naturally  creates a value in multiplicity equal to 1 as a
value that only travels in one direction and does not reciprocate back a
greater value because society is also unpaired. Hint: There is no
multiplicity when there is nothing to pair too.

**Paired:** An individual who is paired also has something to offer as a
member of society which creates a value in multiplicity equal to 2 as a
value that travels in two directions (it reciprocates) between the
paired individual and the pairing community.

**The Pairing Community:** Citizen Gardens is a community of individuals
who were also at one time unpaired, but after much research and
development the ultimate benefits of working together became clear and
too valuable for any one person to claim as their own and that the
benefits should be made available to all completely free of charge.
Forming not just an organizatin, but a community based on appreciating
the value of multiplicity between its members.\
\
**Memberships:** There is no limit to the amount of members this
organization may have and anyone may become a member of this
organization regardless of age, gender, race, religion, financial
orientation, nationality, or affiliation with any other organization,
corporation, or business. Individuals with extensive criminal histories
may become members as long as it is within the confines of the members
local laws and membership programs may be tailored accordingly as the
board of directors may deem necessary. Anyone may participate in this
organization so as long their intention is to not to cause harm or
damages of any kind upon anything or anyone, ultimately adhering to The
U.S. Constitution, The U.S. Declaration of Independence, and The
Universal Declaration of Human Rights.

**Accounts:** Upon pairing (registering) with our community to sponsor,
volunteer, or take part in any program, department, or opportunity an
individual is issued a membership account which serves to outline the
members programs of interest and keep record of their participation.
Every member is issued this handbook with the complete details, rules,
and regulations of their membership and a brochure with a comprehensive
list of services and programs currently available. All members are given
the right to vote in any matter that is directly involved with their
participation and in which they are qualified to do so according to the
terms and conditions contained herein.
