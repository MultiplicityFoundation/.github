**Membership Accounts: **As a member you receive an account which keeps
track of your participation and allows you to easily manage your
membership programs, opportunities, and benefits, accordingly an account
can be credited, debited, and rewarded for a members participation. CGC
serves as a means for us to recognize and reward the efforts and
participation of our individual members and to promote a greater level
of involvement and value within our community.

**Exchanging Rewards:** Members may be provided with the ability to
exchange CGC for local currency under the following rules: 

Children 12 and under may only exchange credits for applicable
services.\
Youth members 13-17 may only exchange credit with parental/guardian
consent up to \$500 a month.\
Members can exchange up to \$1000 a month\
Management can exchange up to \$2000 a month.\
Vice-Presidents and Officers can exchange up to \$3000 a month.\
Presidents can exchange up to \$4000 a month.

**Redeeming Rewards:** There is no limit on the amount of credits a
member can redeem for membership services. 

**Transferring Rewards:** There is no limit to the amount of credits one
member can give to another member.

**Dissolution: **No member may transfer their membership or any right
arising therefrom in whole or in part to anyone except in regards to
transferable rewards. Upon closing a membership in any case these types
of credits can be transferred in whole or in part to another member,
members, or may be dedicated to any charitable purpose within the
organization. Upon dissolution of membership if the total monetary value
of exchangeable credit is more than \$100,000 it will be distributed in
deferred monthly payments unless resolved differently by The Board of
Directors.
