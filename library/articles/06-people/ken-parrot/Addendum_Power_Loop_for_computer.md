Addendum: Photon Recursive Power Loop (PRPL)
Purpose
The Photon Recursive Power Loop (PRPL) is introduced as a turbocharger
to the TriPrime Maximus 98.6 supercomputer. It harnesses recursive Hopf-
stabilized photon cavities to unify three functions:
    1. Continuous local power delivery,
    2. Coherent timing without a global clock,
    3. High-bandwidth photonic interconnect.

Architecture
Each compute tile is augmented with a TriHelix tube-in-tube cavity:
    • Outer crystal annulus — provides refractive stability,
    • Middle gas annulus — enables tunable index control,
    • Inner optical core — sustains recursive photon limit cycles.
A fractal lattice of micro-rings, meso-rings, and macro-rings forms a recursive
resonator hierarchy. Gain media (optical parametric oscillators, semiconductor
amplifiers) maintain oscillations, while Hopf recursion stabilizes flow.

Energy Extraction
Power is harvested non-destructively:
    • Thin-film photovoltaic or rectenna metasurfaces (DC output),
    • Electro-optic modulators (signal tapping),
    • Opto-thermoelectric harvesters (backup).
Extraction efficiency targets 10–25% while maintaining cavity losses below 5%.
