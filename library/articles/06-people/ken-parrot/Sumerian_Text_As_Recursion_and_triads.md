                                       Abstract
          MUL.APIN, the Babylonian star catalog, encodes not only astronomi-
      cal knowledge but also a deeper structural logic based on triadic flow. By
      analyzing Tablet I, Path of Ea (first six entries), through the TriPrime/Trifecta
      framework, we uncover recurring triads of meaning—vision, oral, and mu-
      sical patterns—that reveal how ancient observers synchronized sky, soci-
      ety, and sensory experience. This paper argues that MUL.APIN reflects a
      lawful triadic recursion, anticipating both fractal mathematics and mod-
      ern physics.


2     Introduction
MUL.APIN is both a scientific text (astronomy, calendars) and a cultural text
(ritual, rhythm). The concept of triadic flow highlights recursive structures of
threefold balance (vision–oral–music). Here we show how MUL.APIN’s struc-
ture can be mapped with TriPrime Physics, revealing a unifying law of pattern-
recognition across time.

4     Triadic Flow as Ontological Framework
    • Vision: Sky-mapping, heliacal risings, angular separations.
    • Oral: Textual clauses (after/before, to the right/left, intervals).

    • Music: Rhythmic codings—drums, lyres, flutes—as mnemonic devices

   MUL.APIN encodes not just where stars rise, but how knowledge flows be-
tween sensory channels.

    • Trifecta recursion: growth law T (n) = 3n , mirrored in stellar triads
      across epochs.

    • Lawfulness: prime partitions ensure stable sequencing.


6     Pattern Evidence in MUL.APIN
    • Rising orders form triads with near-equal spacing.

    • Day-intervals cluster around small primes (3, 5, 7).
    • Visibility lengths form balanced triads (a ≈ b ≈ c).

    This suggests intentional encoding of triadic law.
